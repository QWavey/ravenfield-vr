Proposed change: make the button start the same sampling window, run in whatever mode the player is in (not only on foot), keep the 0.3 m floor so seated play still works, and add a status row under "Eye height" in VRSettingsPanel: "measuring, hold still", then "saved 1.62 m" or "not saved: head 0.25 m, too low". Log the same line for both outcomes.
Confidence: high that the two paths differ and that the failure is silent; medium on how often a press lands on a bad sample.

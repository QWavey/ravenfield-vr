1. PLACEHOLDER REPLACER - belt strip is capped at a fixed 0.32 m (about 28 rounds) and stops up to 6 cm short of the hand
Complaint: the magbelt floats in another position (belt-fed guns, the PKM)
File: src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.Parts.cs:331-332, 408, 544, 554; src/Weapons/BeltStrip.cs:13-14, 105
Quote:
  ReloadInteractions.Parts.cs:332    private const float BeltReach = 0.32f;
  ReloadInteractions.Parts.cs:408    if (Vector3.Distance(off.Palm.position, BeltStartWorld) > BeltReach + 0.06f)
  ReloadInteractions.Parts.cs:544    to = from + Vector3.ClampMagnitude(to - from, BeltReach);
  ReloadInteractions.Parts.cs:554    _belt.Place(from, to, Rig.AimWorld, Ammo.MagRounds); // a nearly empty box has only a few rounds of belt
  BeltStrip.cs:13                    private const int MaxRounds = 32; // enough for the whole length the belt comes out of the box
  BeltStrip.cs:14                    private const float Pitch = 0.0115f; // link spacing of a rifle-calibre belt
What is wrong: the length of the drawn belt is a constant, not the belt in the box. The strip end is clamped to 0.32 m from the box mouth (544), but the hand only lets go at 0.38 m (408). Between 32 and 38 cm the strip stops up to 6 cm short of the fingers. The link pitch is one fixed 11.5 mm for every belt. Ammo.MagRounds only lowers the count (554), so a full box never shows more than about 28 rounds. The comment on BeltReach ("about 28 rounds") and the comment on MaxRounds ("the whole length the belt comes out of the box") describe the constant, not a measurement. Tasks.md:1427 documents BeltReach as the belt reach, but not the 6 cm release gap or the 28-round limit as faults.
Trigger / what the player sees: pull the belt out past 32 cm from the box mouth: the drawn strip stops in the air short of the hand while the fingers keep going, then the belt drops off at 38 cm. A full box shows the same 28 rounds as a short one.
Proposed change: take the reach from the measured belt (the belt part's length on the MG3-style guns, or the belt's rounds in the reload trajectory) and the pitch from the belt's own round spacing. Clamp to that measured reach and use the same value for the release distance (408), so the strip end always meets the hand.
Confidence: medium. The clamp at 544 and the release at 408 are read in full, and the 6 cm gap follows from them. Not checked in game. The 11.5 mm pitch is a comment claim, not a measurement in this code.

What is wrong: when OpenVR's display frequency cannot be read and Unity's refreshRate is 1 Hz or less, the budget is a made 90 Hz. Trigger / what the player sees: a headset whose refresh is not 90 Hz, with the OpenVR read failing. At 72 Hz the vsync frame is 13.9 ms, above the 13.3 ms threshold (1.2 x 11.1 ms), so every frame counts as late and the preset steps down although the headset is on time. Or measure the rate from the median frame interval over the first seconds of play instead of the constant.
Confidence: medium on the code: the fallback, the threshold and the hold rule are all read. Low for the user's own setup, because the guess matches the rate in use there.

WF-analyzer-core-0  Major (sure)  src/Weapons/WeaponAnalyzer.cs:428
     An arm-named transform with no mesh of its own (a group such as Scope_Rings) is kept as an arm bone by name alone, and arm-named meshes under it are kept as arm meshes too, so the group's ancestors (receiver and gun body) become arm-related.
     Trigger: A gun whose model has a mesh-less group with an arm word (ring, middle, index, palm, finger, thumb, wrist, arm, shoulder, knuckle, elbow, little) under its receiver, not on the arms' skeleton. Example: group Scope_Rings holding meshes Ring_L and Ring_R. Ring_L is name-matched (line 406); LoneArmNamedMesh returns false for it because its named parent counts as an arm chain (Helpers.cs:259-261), and the group fails the MeshFilter/MeshRenderer test (Helpers.cs:253), so both stay in named. Consequences: (1) line 520-523 hides Ring_L's MeshRenderer as an arm mesh, so the part vanishes in the headset; (2) lines 468-475 add the group's ancestors (the receiver and gun body) to armRelated; (3) the gun-body climb at lines 561-569 stops at the first armRelated ancestor of the muzzle, so the gun body is taken as a sub-part, and IsPartCandidate (Helpers.cs:87) rejects the receiver. The comment at lines 411-413 describes this receiver-excluded failure and was fixed only for transforms that own a mesh. Frequency depends on mod naming; the mechanism is certain from the code.
     Fix: Treat a name-matched transform as an arm only when it is in armListed or on an arm chain (the way OnArmChain is used for listed bones at line 462), not on its name alone. Alternatively, in LoneArmNamedMesh, do not let a non-armListed named parent make a mesh an arm chain member, and apply the same no-arm-subtree test to groups. Add a regression case for a mesh-less Scope_Rings group under the receiver.
     STATUS: open - measured first (6c49d9e: 'arm-named group' note in the self-test); change after a run
WF-analyzer-core-1  Minor (unsure)  src/Weapons/PoseSampler.cs:824
     EventTracker.Scan treats every clip as looping. A non-looping clip's events are re-fired at each loop boundary while its state is still current.
     Trigger: A non-looping fire or reload clip whose state is still current past its end, either crossfading out (the source state's normalizedTime keeps running past 1) or held without an exit. The loop-crossing loop at lines 824-828 has no clip.isLooping check, so an event at offset e from the clip start, for example a gunshot PlaySoundBank at about t=0, is re-added at 1.0 clip length plus e. Example: length 0.5 s, event at 0.01 s, from 0.48 s to now 0.60 s, gives a cue at 0.51 s. Effects: a Manual gun gets a bogus SlideBack or SlideForward cue (WeaponAnalyzer.Sounds.cs:49-63, cues later than 0.1 s with no earlier slide cue), and an extra reload cue shifts the sorted slot order (Sounds.cs:32-45, whose comment says an extra cue shifts later slots).
     Fix: Check clip.isLooping. For non-looping clips only the first pass counts (loopStart = 0, and et must be within clip.length), so the start-of-clip event is not re-added at the end.
     STATUS: fixed (71656b2: non-looping clips fire their events once)

1. [minor] [medium] muzzle_flash_or_lens_flash_issue  (lens-eyes-040-M1014 Tactical.png)
   seen: Both eye panels (left x 0-300, right x 303-606, 250 px tall) are almost all black. Each shows one small orange star about 6-8 px across at roughly 56% across and 58% down, with a faint ring around it that is barely visible. Left and right are about equal; the right may be a touch brighter. The position matches the muzzle in lensflash-040 (about 54% across, 57% down), so placement is right. In the close-ups the hand stays on the handle bump (fist in rack-grip-left vs rack-grip-pulled-left; finger on the top shelf in rack-grip-right vs rack-grip-pulled-right), which is plausible but cannot be measured from pictures.
  - '2 drawn part(s) move, most 57 mm; the nearest is 0.0 cm from the hand': in the pulled-left close-up the receiver and sight shift about 200 px on screen while the fist and the handle bump stay put. At close range that is roughly 5-6 cm, so the picture is roughly consistent with a 57 mm handle travel. The before and after framing differs, so this is not proof, and the direction (rear or forward) cannot be read from the frames.
  - 'the gun moved 0.5 mm with the pull': the before and after close-ups are not framed the same (receiver about 200 px shift), so the pictures cannot check the 0.5 mm figure.
  - 'muzzle flash: particles Muzzle Flash Particles, 200 cm': in lensflash-040 the muzzle flash is a small spark cluster roughly 5-10 cm across at the muzzle, not a 2 m flash. The 200 cm figure does not match what the picture shows; it may be a particle setting in another unit or a range. Picture does not confirm it.
  - 'lens flash shown, strength 0.08': matches the very faint flare in lens-eyes-040 (see the bug above).
  - The overview sheet shows 'none' for mag 1-7. That is expected for the M1014, a tube-fed shotgun with no detachable magazine, so it is not a bug.

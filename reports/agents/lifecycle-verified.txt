#1 Evicted gun volumes leave hull Meshes alive (minor)
VERDICT: CONFIRMED
- GunVolume.cs:282-288 removes evicted entries from Cache/CacheOrder and destroys nothing. Hull Pieces come from ShapeFit.FitCloud (GunVolume.cs:557); Piece.Mesh creates a `new Mesh` lazily (ShapeFit.cs:34-36), used by AddColliders (ShapeFit.cs:605). GunCollider.Destroy (GunCollider.cs:842-853) only destroys _go. Nothing calls ShapeFit.ClearCache (ShapeFit.cs:648).
- Trigger is reachable: CacheSize = 12 (GunVolume.cs:24), and the HeldWeapon path (HeldWeapon.cs:1076 -> GunVolume.For, GunVolume.cs:227 sets WantsHull) feeds hull pieces into colliders (HeldWeapon.cs:1221/1228/1236/1249).
- Caveat on severity: a runtime `new Mesh` is native memory that Unity frees on Resources.UnloadUnusedAssets. Unity runs that on scene loads, so the leak may last only until the next level load, not "the rest of the session". Severity should be tiny to minor.
- Fix idea: destroy HullPieces[i].Mesh when the volume leaves the cache, or cache hull meshes by weapon key.

[minor/high] src/Weapons/BeltStrip.cs:83  Belt strip is re-laid and re-uploaded every frame after a belt is laid by hand
  what:   ReloadInteractions.UpdateBeltStrip (ReloadInteractions.cs:891-936) ends with `_belt.Place(from, to, Rig.AimWorld, Ammo.MagRounds);` and runs every frame from BeforeRender (1618, call at 1663) whenever `show` is true. `_beltShownLaid = _held.BeltPartCount == 0;` (869) is cleared only when the magazine is out, the gun has no cover, or the cover is open over an unlaid belt (893-909). After the cover is shut it stays true for as long as the gun is held. Place (BeltStrip.cs:83-103) loops all MaxRounds=32, writing 99 vertices and 99 normals per round (rounds beyond `count` are still written as zeros), then assigns `_mesh.vertices` and `_mesh.normals` (104-105, about 38 KB each) and calls RecalculateBounds (106).
  why:    For a belt-fed gun whose model has no belt part (BeltPartCount == 0) with a belt laid by hand (BeltByHand defaults to true, VRConfig.cs:275), that is about 6,300 quaternion-vector transforms, about 76 KB of mesh upload and a bounds scan every frame, even when the box holds two rounds. Estimate 0.1-0.2 ms a frame for as long as the gun is held (not measured). The mechanism is certain; the magnitude is rough.
  change: Parent `_root` to the gun body (Rig.GunBody) and place the strip once in gun-local space when its inputs change (round count or belt state), so it moves with the gun for free. If it must stay in world space, skip Place when from/to/barrel are unchanged within about 1 mm and 0.5 degrees, upload only the first `count` rounds, and set `_mesh.bounds` instead of calling RecalculateBounds.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/MagazineProp.cs:687  Each magazine copy re-bakes the gun skin and rescans its triangles, with no cache
  what:   Create (366-398) -> CheckedCopy -> CopyVisuals -> CopyVisualsShown (570-666) calls `smr.BakeMesh(mesh)` for each skinned magazine part (630-634). For skinned guns, ExtractBoneTriangles (687-764) bakes the whole gun mesh (`smr.BakeMesh(baked)`, 712) and loops every triangle of every submesh with DrivenBy, growing Dictionary and List copies (716-746).   why:    Runs once per magazine made (taken from the pouch, or copied for a loose prop) on the main thread during the reload action. Cost scales with the gun's triangle count. Not measured; likely several ms on a dense skinned gun. It is a hitch, not a steady cost.

Or prepare the copy when the weapon is drawn (GunVolume.For already does its work then) rather than on the reload frame.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/MagazineProp.cs:1566  Grab search runs ClosestPoint on every dropped magazine, no bounds pre-check
  what:   DistanceTo: `if (_colliders[0].enabled && Go.activeInHierarchy) { foreach (Collider c in _colliders) best = Mathf.Min(best, Vector3.Distance(c.ClosestPoint(point), point)); }`.   why:    Runs every frame while a free hand has GrabPressed or GrabReaching (ReloadInteractions.cs:168, WeaponSystem.cs:185; VRHand.cs:261 counts a press from the last 0.25 s), so for as long as the grab button is held.   change: In DistanceTo, before the ClosestPoint loop, skip any piece whose box is already beyond the cap: `Vector3 local = Go.transform.InverseTransformPoint(point); for each i: if (_pieceBounds[i].SqrDistance(local) >= cap*cap) continue;` (the held branch at 1572-1576 already measures against these boxes).

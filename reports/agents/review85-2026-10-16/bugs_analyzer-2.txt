[major/medium] src/Weapons/WeaponAnalyzer.cs:2038  Name fallback can pick the other gun's magazine in dual rigs
  what:   Line 2038: `mag = FindMagByName(all, root, gunBody, muzzle, armRelated, gunBones);`. FindMagByName (line 3078) tests only `IsMagName(t.name) && IsPartCandidate(...)`. The reload searches use MagCandidate (line 2591), which rejects parts under a SecondWeaponName ancestor ("dual|akimbo|off hand|second pistol") and parts where LooksLikeAWeapon is true. The MagCandidate comment ("part of the other gun of a pair... Taking it for the magazine dropped the second pistol on every reload") describes this exact failure.
  why:    In a dual/akimbo rig where this gun's magazine is neither moved nor hidden by the reload, the fallback returns the first magazine-named part in hierarchy order. If the second pistol's branch comes first, that magazine becomes this gun's MagPath and MagSeat. The wrong magazine is then ejected, copied and used for insertion, and this gun's real magazine never moves.
  change: Pass `slide` into FindMagByName and test `MagCandidate(t, root, gunBody, muzzle, slide, armRelated, gunBones)` (keep the IsMagName check) instead of `IsPartCandidate(...)` in its loop at line 3078-3085.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:235  Grab reach scans every dropped magazine twice per frame, calling ClosestPoint each time
  what:   TryGrab evaluates `MagazineProp.NearestDropped(palm, 0.13f, Data.Key) ?? MagazineProp.NearestDropped(palm, 0.13f, null)` (lines 233-235). TryGrab runs from Update (line 170) every frame while the grip is reaching (GrabReaching, a 0.25 s window after each squeeze, line 168). Each NearestDropped walks MagazineProp.Alive and calls DistanceTo (MagazineProp.cs:1611-1629), which calls Collider.ClosestPoint once per collider of each dropped prop (MagazineProp.cs:1561-1567). A reach that finds nothing runs both passes. The default MaxDroppedMags is 12 (VRConfig.cs:223).
  why:    Up to about 24 dropped-prop scans, with ClosestPoint per collider, per frame for roughly 11-22 frames per squeeze (depending on frame rate), but only near empty space or other magazines. Estimated tens of microseconds with box colliders, more with mesh colliders. Not frame-breaking; not measured.
  change: Walk MagazineProp.All once and track the best same-key candidate and the best any-key candidate in the same pass, which halves the scans. Also skip ClosestPoint for any prop whose cached bounding sphere (centre and radius from its renderer bounds at creation) is more than 0.13 m beyond the reach radius.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:935  Belt strip rebuilds all 32 round slots every frame while a laid belt is shown
  what:   UpdateBeltStrip keeps the strip shown after a belt is laid on a gun with no belt part (CompleteBelt sets `_beltShownLaid = _held.BeltPartCount == 0;` at line 869; line 910 `bool show = _beltHeld || _beltHanging || _beltShownLaid;`) and calls `_belt.Place(from, to, Rig.AimWorld, Ammo.MagRounds);` at line 935 on every call. BeltStrip.Place (src/Weapons/BeltStrip.cs:83-106) loops all MaxRounds = 32 rounds (BeltStrip.cs:13), writes 99 vertices and 99 normals per round via `rot * _roundVerts[k]` and `rot * _roundNormals[k]` (lines 100-101), then uploads `_mesh.vertices` and `_mesh.normals` and calls RecalculateBounds() (lines 104-106). UpdateBeltStrip is called from BeforeRender (ReloadInteractions.cs:1663), which runs every frame.
  why:    About 3,170 vertices a frame: roughly 6,300 Quaternion-times-Vector3 multiplies (a native call in Unity 2020.3), two 3,170-element mesh uploads and a bounds pass. Estimated 0.15-0.3 ms a frame (about 1.5-3% of the 11 ms budget). It applies for as long as the box is seated, the belt is laid and the gun model has no belt part, even when the gun is still. Not measured.
  change: Keep the last inputs in gun space: from = Rig.ToGun(BeltStartWorld), to = Rig.ToGun(FeedTrayWorld), the barrel direction in gun space, and the round count. When the belt is laid or hanging and those inputs are unchanged, skip the vertex loop and both uploads (BeltStrip.cs:83-106) and only move the root transform to the gun pose each frame (SetPositionAndRotation). Rewrite and upload the round slots only when the gun-space inputs or the count change.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:1243  Ungated, formatted Log.Info about three times a second while a magazine sits in the strike window
  what:   TryMagKick calls `Log.Info($"Mag kick test {Data.Key}: along {along * 100f:0.0} cm, lateral ... rounds {prop.Rounds}")` every 0.3 s whenever the held magazine is in the strike window (`near`, lines 1238-1244). The message is formatted before the call. Unlike the Log.Debug helper, which is gated by VRConfig.DebugLogging (Log.cs:18-24; the flag defaults to false, VRConfig.cs:330), this call is not gated.
  why:    A magazine held near the well (lining up a fresh one, or hovering there) produces a formatted, synchronous log write about three times a second for as long as it stays there, at default settings. That is a periodic main-thread hitch of unknown size (it depends on BepInEx's listeners; not verified from the repo) plus a small boxing allocation per call.
  change: Add `VRConfig.DebugLogging.Value &&` to the `if` on line 1239 (or switch to Log.Debug and guard the string build the same way), so the formatting and write happen only with VerboseLogging on. Keep the existing 0.3 s rate limit.

[tiny/high] src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:281  Sight-mode key lookup runs before the cheap optic distance test on every grab attempt
  what:   TryGrab tests `if (HasSwitchableSight)` (line 281) before the cheap `OpticDistance(palm)` (line 283). For most weapons HasAnyAltSightModes() is false (Weapon.cs:777-784 returns false when sightModes is null or empty), so the `||` falls through to `Input.ScriptKeys.Find(w, ScriptKeyPurpose.Magnifier)`. That calls KeysOn (src/Input/ScriptKeys.cs:295-315), which allocates a List, walks every key dictionary in the game-wide `Seen`, calls IsChildOf per key and sorts.
  why:    On every TryGrab call in the grab-reach window, for most weapons: a few microseconds and a few hundred bytes of garbage per frame, even when the hand is nowhere near the optic.
  change: Compute the distance first: `float d = OpticDistance(palm); if (d < 0.06f && HasSwitchableSight) { Consider(8, ...) }`. Do not cache HasSwitchableSight across frames, because magnifier script keys register at runtime (ScriptKeys.Seen).

[tiny/high] src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:1046  SeatPose and HoldPose are recomputed three times a frame while a magazine is held
  what:   UpdateHeldProp calls TryMagKick (line 1032), which computes SeatPose (1219), HoldPose (1223) and two ThrowVelocity() calls (1231). UpdateHeldProp then recomputes SeatPose (1046) and HoldPose (1048), and HandAlongRail (1049) computes both again (1166, 1169). So SeatPose and HoldPose each run three times per frame, and ThrowVelocity twice.
  why:    SeatPose reads the GunBody transform and may do an AngleAxis (hinge guns); HoldPose does two LookRotation, two Quaternion.Inverse and a Lerp. Estimated 5-10 microseconds a frame in total. Tiny, but it is repeated work with no benefit.
  change: Compute seat, hold and the two throw velocities once at the top of UpdateHeldProp and pass them into TryMagKick, plus an overload of HandAlongRail that takes the seat and hold poses.

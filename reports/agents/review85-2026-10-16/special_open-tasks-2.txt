[major/medium] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:643  Launcher jitter: hand acceleration differenced twice from reported velocity feeds a 12/s spring
  what:   StepInertia: `Vector3 raw = dt > 1e-4f ? (v - _handVelPrev) / dt : Vector3.zero;` then `_handAcc = Vector3.Lerp(_handAcc, raw, 1f - Mathf.Exp(-25f * dt));`, which drives the position spring (line 654) and the pitch spring (line 660: `wr * wr * _inertiaLever * ... * Vector3.Dot(_handAcc, Vector3.up)`). Launchers take this path in one hand: `omega = 12f; lever = 0.6f; // launchers` (line 605). `v` is main.Velocity, the runtime's reported controller velocity (VRHand.cs:124).
  why:    Frame-to-frame noise in the reported velocity becomes an acceleration of order 1 m/s^2 after differencing. At 0.6 degrees of pitch per m/s^2 and about 1/omega^2 of position, a still hand gets a sustained wobble of a few tenths of a degree and millimetres, which is the RPG shake of 203 with no firing. The candidates listed in 203 do not produce frame-to-frame motion: the droop is a static offset (line 685, `sag = 8f`) and the trail is a low-pass (line 1084, Slerp at `_followRate`). The shouldered check has hysteresis (lines 70-71).
  change: Add a deadband to raw (zero it below about 0.5 m/s^2) and lower the 25/s cut-off in line 646 for launchers, or gate the inertia off while the hand's speed stays below about 0.05 m/s for 0.3 s. Confirm with the autotest's held-still measurement against the 203 target (under 0.2 degrees and 2 mm).

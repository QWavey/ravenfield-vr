[major/medium] src/Rig/DamageFeedback.cs:76  Directional hit buzz is issued first, then overlapped by the symmetric buzz
  what:   OnHitDirection buzzes the side hand: rig.Hand(side > 0f ? Input.Hand.Right : Input.Hand.Left).Haptic(0.07f, 70f, Mathf.Clamp01(0.4f + Mathf.Abs(side) * 0.5f)) (lines 76-78). The game calls ShowDamageIndicator synchronously inside ReceivedDamage (FpsActorController.cs:658; DamageUI.cs:68-72 applies it at once). The Harmony postfix of ReceivedDamage then calls OnDamage in the same frame, which buzzes every hand with h.Haptic(0.06f + amp * 0.08f, 55f, amp) (line 94). VRInput.Haptic issues one TriggerHapticVibrationAction per call with no merging (VRInput.cs:547).
  why:    The directional pulse is not the last one sent to the side hand. If a later haptic call on the same controller replaces an earlier one (the code does not show SteamVR's rule), the side hand loses its 70 Hz directional pulse and every hit feels symmetric. Hits from straight ahead or behind (|side| <= 0.3) never get a directional pulse. The directional pulse is also a fixed 0.07 s whatever the damage, while OnDamage scales by damage (line 88).
  change: Have OnHitDirection store the hit's side instead of buzzing. OnDamage, which runs right after it in the same call, then gives the side hand one directional pulse (0.07 s, 70 Hz, amplitude from |side| and the damage amp) and the other hand the symmetric pulse. Each hand gets one pulse. Front and back hits keep the symmetric pulse only.

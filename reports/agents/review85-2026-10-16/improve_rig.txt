[minor/medium] src/Rig/CockpitControls.cs:259  Wheel steering gain depends on where the rim is grabbed
  what:   Line 259 `return axial < 0.12f && radial < 0.32f;` accepts a palm anywhere within 32 cm of the hub. OnWheelPlane (lines 264-265) takes the palm's direction from the hub, and AddTurn measures the sweep with Vector3.SignedAngle (line 216).
  why:    A hand's angle around the hub is arc divided by radius, so the same 1 cm of hand motion turns the rim about 11 degrees at 5 cm from the hub and about 3 degrees at a 19 cm rim radius: steering several times faster when grabbed near the hub. Under about 1 cm (the 1e-6 test at line 265) millimetre tracker noise swings the direction by several degrees per frame. SteeringWheel.cs has no radius field, so 0.32 and 0.12 are magic numbers.
  change: Accept grabs only near the rim: replace `radial < 0.32f` with `Mathf.Abs(radial - rimRadius) < 0.12f`, where rimRadius is measured in game from the wheel's renderer bounds (GetComponentInChildren<Renderer>().bounds.extents) or set as a constant. This keeps the steering gain constant and removes the hub jitter zone.

  what:   Line 219 `Mathf.Clamp(0.25f + 0.2f * kick, 0.2f, 0.8f)` reaches its ceiling at kick 2.75. ShotKick (HeldWeapon.cs lines 914-938) returns 0.5 to 10, a launcher's floor is 1.2 (line 932), and its round term alone can reach 5.25.

[major/medium] src/Rig/VRHand.cs:158  Free glove drawn with no arm, idle at a fixed spot in the grass
  what:   The simulated idle hand sits at a fixed rig offset: var offset = gunHand ? (0.16,-0.26,0.40) : (0.08,-0.29,0.62) (VRHand.cs:158). ArmIK is created only for the weapon's own arm (HeldWeapon.cs:190). A grep of HandModel.cs, GloveMesh.cs, VRHand.cs and HandPoses.cs for arm or forearm finds nothing for the free hand.
  why:    part_floating_or_detached is 29 items on 28 guns. 30 summary entries place the idle glove at x 540-635, y 585-690, in the grass, and 5 say explicitly there is no forearm or arm (e.g. 045 SAWED-OFF, 065 RPG-7V OG-7V, 150 K400, 169 BRN180 S A S). The glove looks detached in the game too, because it is drawn at the real controller with nothing behind it.
  change: Give the free hand a forearm: a short tube from the glove's wrist toward the lower edge of the view, or run ArmIK's solve for the off arm (the same model as HeldWeapon.cs:190). Alternatively fade the glove once it is far from the head. Design decision for the user; the test pose itself is a pose choice.

[major/medium] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:1540  Racking fingers stay straight when no part solid is measured
  what:   PartSolid() returns null when WorkedPart is null or no renderer mesh is measured (1540-1550). RackSolid() returns null when there is no part and no bolt piece (1590-1596), and off.Model.Solid is set only when part != null (1708-1711). HandModel.Closing needs Solid != null (HandModel.cs:796-797), and the curl branch at HandModel.cs:1256-1279 is skipped without it.
  why:    With Solid null the racking glove keeps the pose's open shape: the fingers lie straight across the receiver or float beside the part instead of wrapping the charging handle or bolt. 54 rack-grip entries describe straight or flat fingers (e.g. 022 QUICKSILVER, 071 92gs, 130 ak12 1p87s, 159 PKM Z SP S, 265 AF2011A1). The self-test label 'not closing (no solid)' (HandModel.cs:300) is the same state. I did not confirm which guns hit the null branch.
  change: When a racking hand has no part solid, fall back to a small capsule or ball around RackPointWorld (as the drawn-handle branch at HeldWeapon.cs:1595-1606 already does for a bolt piece) so Closing is true and the fingers wrap. Log the guns that take the fallback, so the count can be checked on fresh pictures.

  why:    44 of 83 mag-5-lined-up entries show the magazine on the grass with no gun or magwell in frame, which reads as a magazine that fell out.

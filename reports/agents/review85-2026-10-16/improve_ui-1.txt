[major/high] src/UI/VRInputModule.cs:163  Hover tick feels as strong as a click on Oculus Touch
  what:   HoverTick fires `VRInput.Haptic(PointerHand, 0.006f, 180f, 0.12f);` up to 20 times a second (lines 160-163). The press fires `VRInput.Haptic(PointerHand, 0.01f, 200f, 0.25f);` (line 296). VRInput.Haptic floors every pulse on Touch controllers: duration to 0.045 s, amplitude to 0.35 x strength, frequency to 320 Hz (src/Input/VRInput.cs:538-540).
  why:    On Quest/Rift Touch a hover pulse becomes a 45 ms, 0.35, 320 Hz buzz, the same as the click pulse. Sweeping the laser across a menu gives up to 20 buzzes a second, and hovering feels exactly like pressing. On Index-type controllers the same call stays faint, so the feel differs by headset.
  change: Skip the hover pulse for Touch hands: make VRInput.IsTouch (src/Input/VRInput.cs:560, currently private) internal and add `!VRInput.IsTouch(PointerHand)` to the condition in HoverTick. Keep the press pulse. Alternatively cut the hover rate for Touch to about 8 a second.

[minor/medium] src/UI/UISystem.cs:61  One-Euro laser filter estimates speed from filter lag, so its cutoff depends on frame rate
  what:   `_laserDirVelocity = Vector3.Lerp(_laserDirVelocity, (raw - _laserDir) / dt, SmoothingAlpha(1f, dt));` (line 61). `_laserDir` is the filtered direction (line 63), so `raw - _laserDir` is the filter lag, which is about v x tau with tau = 1/(2 pi cutoff), not the change since the last sample. The cutoff at line 62 is `LaserMinCutoff + LaserBeta * _laserDirVelocity.magnitude * Mathf.Rad2Deg`.
  why:    The speed estimate is inflated by tau/dt, so the cutoff follows the square root of the frame rate. At 1 deg/s the filter runs at about 4 Hz at 90 Hz (designed value 2 Hz), and the value changes between 72 and 120 Hz headsets. The tuning in the comment at lines 39-46 was measured with this code at one rate, so re-measure after changing it.
  change: Keep the previous raw sample (`_laserRawPrev`), use `(raw - _laserRawPrev) / dt` as the derivative (standard One-Euro), and reset it wherever `_laserDir` resets (lines 55-58). Then re-run the tremor measurement from the comment at lines 39-41 at 72, 90 and 120 Hz.

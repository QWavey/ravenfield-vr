  what:   Each pass over all reads t.name again and runs its own regex: e.g. ArmName/MagName/SlideName at line 292, TriggerName.IsMatch(t.name) at 1054, RoundName.IsMatch(t.name) at 1058, and ChargingName, CameraName and others in the later passes.

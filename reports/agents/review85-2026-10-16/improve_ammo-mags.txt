[major/high] src/Weapons/MagazineProp.cs:380  Rocket insert override makes front parallel to insert: held roll and stand-in orientation become arbitrary
  what:   Create() (lines 375-380) sets insertGun = -d.AimAxis.normalized when a rocket's eject axis is off the barrel, but line 384 still sets frontLocal = seatInv * d.AimAxis.   why:    HoldPose (line 942) rotates the rocket by LookRotation(FrontLocal, InsertDirLocal), so every rocket taken from the pouch or the well is held at a fixed roll that is not the barrel's (for a barrel along +z in seat space the fallback is -y, 90 degrees off). [minor/low] src/Weapons/AmmoCounter.cs:40  On-gun round counter is fixed in size and placement for every weapon, and much smaller than the magazine readout
  what:   _text.characterSize = 0.0009f (line 40) with fontSize 64; the offset GripPoint + Up*0.045f - right*0.035f + AimAxis*0.02f (line 36) is also a constant. MagReadout uses characterSize 0.0024 and says in its comment (MagReadout.cs:49) that this gives about 1.5 cm digits.
  why:    Scaling that comment linearly, the gun counter's digits are about 0.5 cm, roughly 0.5 degrees tall from a rifle held at arm's length. That is the main count during a fight and it is harder to read than the readout that is shown on the magazine.   change: Derive characterSize and the offset from the weapon (for example from the receiver's bounds in WeaponRigData) so the digits keep a fixed angular size, or raise the constant toward about 0.0015 and check on a pistol and a rifle with the self-test.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/MagazineProp.cs:214  Magazine mass is fixed by kind and ignores the rounds it holds
  what:   _body.mass = kind == PropKind.Rocket ? 2f : machineGun && kind == PropKind.Magazine ? 1.5f : 0.4f; is set once in the constructor. Rounds changes later (SeatProp, ReturnRounds, Despawn at line 1607), but the mass never does.
  why:    A full 30-round magazine and an empty one both weigh 0.4 kg on the drop, and a pistol magazine is as heavy as a rifle magazine. Throws and bounces feel the same whatever is loaded, which the player will notice when a full box and an empty one land.
  change: For magazines use a mass that follows the rounds, for example mass = 0.12f + 0.012f * Rounds (placeholder estimate; tune to the weapon), and recompute it in Drop() and wherever Rounds is changed for a dropped prop. Keep the fixed values for rockets and shells.

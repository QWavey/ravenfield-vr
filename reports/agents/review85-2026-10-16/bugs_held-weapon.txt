[minor/low] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:52  Supported ignores whether the off hand is tracked, unlike the two-hand blend
  what:   `public bool Supported => TwoHanded || _system.Rig.Off.Role == HandRole.Foregrip;` (line 52). The two-hand steering blend, by contrast, requires `TwoHanded && off.Tracked` (line 1042). TwoHanded is set and cleared only by the reload module (ReloadInteractions.cs:190, 346, 1498, 1571, 3035), and nothing clears it when the off controller drops tracking.
  why:    While TwoHanded is still true and the off hand is untracked, Supported is true. That turns off the droop (sagWant at line 1068), applies the two-hand recoil factors (873-874), uses the stiffer recoil spring (1005), and gives inertia 2.2x (648), while the gun is steered one-handed.
  change: `public bool Supported => _system.Rig.Off.Tracked && (TwoHanded || _system.Rig.Off.Role == HandRole.Foregrip);`. AutoTest drives simulated grabs and may report Tracked as false, so check the self-tests after the change.

[minor/high] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:672  Knives and grenades get gun droop and weight lag from the rifle or SMG rows
  what:   Stock detection excludes Melee and Throwable explicitly (line 173: `rig.Data.Action != ActionType.Melee && rig.Data.Action != ActionType.Throwable`), and ClassOf gives them their own classes (lines 218-238). Handling (672-699) and Inertia (592-619) have no such exclusion. A knife with a blade under 0.32 m takes the SMG row (`sag = 3f; follow = 22f;` at line 693; Inertia omega 26), a longer one the rifle row (sag 5, omega 20). The constructor calls both unconditionally (164-165). BeforeRender droops the gun by _sagDegrees whenever it is held one-handed (1068-1081), and StepInertia lags it (1089).
  why:    A knife held in one hand droops 3-5 degrees and lags the hand; a grenade in hand gets the same. About 1.6 cm of drop at the tip of a 30 cm blade. ThrowAndMelee.cs:589-591 samples the blade from this same placement, so hit detection moves with the droop. The class-based tables were written for guns ('Pistols are made for one hand; long and heavy guns are not', line 575-577).
  change: At the top of Handling and Inertia, add `if (d.Action == ActionType.Melee || d.Action == ActionType.Throwable)` and set sag and follow to 0 (Handling) and omega and lever to 0 (Inertia). Or give these actions explicit rows. Keep ClassOf as is, since the hand poses use it. Confirm the intent first: no comment says melee or throwables should have weight.

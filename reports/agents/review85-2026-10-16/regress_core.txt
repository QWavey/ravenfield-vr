[minor/high] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:881  TwoHandRecoilReduction default change also raises one-handed long-gun recoil by 50%
  what:   `strength *= Rig.Data.IsPistol ? _oneHandRecoil : _oneHandRecoil * (1f - VRConfig.TwoHandRecoilReduction.Value);` applies to every non-supported gun. The setting is documented as "Fraction of recoil removed while holding the foregrip" (src/Core/VRConfig.cs:240). The window changed its default from 0.6 to 0.4 (VRConfig.cs:239) with a revision-5 migration (VRConfig.cs:381-389) whose comment only describes the two-hand feel. _oneHandRecoil is 1.9 for rifles (HeldWeapon.cs:697), 1.5 for SMGs (693) and 2.6 for machine guns (681).
  why:    On upgrade, for players still on the default, one-handed long guns get a 0.6 multiplier instead of 0.4: rifles 0.76 to 1.14, SMGs 0.60 to 0.90, machine guns 1.04 to 1.56 (+50%), with no mention in the migration. At TwoHandRecoilReduction = 1 a one-handed rifle would get zero kick. Two-handed rifles going from 0.4x to 0.6x is the intended change; the one-handed side is an unannounced side effect of the same setting.
  change: Make the one-hand factor independent of the foregrip setting. To keep the old one-hand feel: `strength *= Rig.Data.IsPistol ? _oneHandRecoil : _oneHandRecoil * 0.4f;`. If the stronger one-hand kick is wanted, use `_oneHandRecoil` and say so in the migration comment at VRConfig.cs:381-383.

[major/medium] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:1177  Magnified sight re-renders the whole scene a second time every frame while aiming
  what:   BeforeRender calls `_scope.BeforeRender(Aiming);` every frame (line 1177; the mode lenses do the same at line 1206). VRScope.BeforeRender (src/Weapons/VRScope.cs:259-270) sets `_camera.enabled = true` and rewrites fieldOfView, cullingMask, clearFlags, backgroundColor, farClipPlane, allowHDR and depthTextureMode every frame. The camera renders into a RenderTexture sized by VRConfig.ScopeResolution (default 768, src/Core/VRConfig.cs:247-248) with antiAliasing = 2 (VRScope.cs:77). Its cullingMask is the headset camera's mask minus layers 5 and 18, so it draws the whole scene.
  why:    While Aiming, or while the eye is within 22 cm of the lens (VRScope.cs:252), each frame pays a second cull, draw and shadow pass on top of the headset's own render at 90 fps. Estimate, not measured: roughly 1 to 3 ms GPU and under 1 ms CPU on a mid-range PC. That is a large share of the 11 ms budget during an ACOG or sniper hold. The six camera setters also run every frame.
  change: In VRScope.BeforeRender keep `_camera.enabled = false` and call `_camera.Render()` on alternate frames (`Time.frameCount % 2 == 0`) to halve the cost. Or lower the ScopeResolution default in VRConfig.cs:247 from 768 to 512 (about 44% fewer pixels). Write the six camera settings only when the headset camera's values differ from the last ones written. Check the lens picture at 45 Hz in the headset before keeping the halving, since it may judder.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:121  Every draw creates a new scope RenderTexture, camera and materials
  what:   `_scope = new VRScope(system.Rig, rig);` (line 121) and the per-mode lenses created on first selection (line 1199) each build a RenderTexture (VRScope.cs:77-78), a camera, three materials and an Instantiate'd mesh (VRScope.cs:87-107). Release() destroys them all (HeldWeapon.cs:2131-2138; VRScope.Destroy at VRScope.cs:295-305 releases the texture).
  why:    Not per frame. Each draw of a scoped gun, and each magnified mode the first time it is selected in that draw, allocates and releases a 768x768 MSAA render target and several GameObjects on the main thread. A hitch per draw, size not measured.
  change: Keep VRScope objects in a static cache keyed by weapon key plus mode index, the way GunVolume caches its measurements (src/Weapons/GunVolume.cs:24-27). On Release only hide them (SetShown(false)) instead of destroying them. On reuse, re-attach the lens and camera to the current rig and check the eye point and radius still match.

[minor/low] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:1256  First draw of a weapon type: scene-wide renderer scan and main-thread view rendering
  what:   `_volume = GunVolume.For(Rig, frame, Rig.Data.GripPoint);` (line 1256) runs on the first draw of each weapon type. On a cache miss, GunVolume.Build calls FreeLayer (src/Weapons/GunVolume.cs:429), which runs `UnityEngine.Object.FindObjectsOfType<Renderer>()` (GunVolume.cs:361) once per scene. Build then renders its views on the main thread (GunVolume.cs:540, RenderMs).
  why:    A one-off hitch on the first weapon drawn after a map loads. FindObjectsOfType over every renderer in a Ravenfield map is a full scene walk, plausibly several ms and possibly tens of ms on a large map (not measured). It is cached afterwards, so it is not a per-frame cost.
  change: Compute the free layer once when the scene loads, before the player can draw a weapon, rather than on the first draw. Or move the scan into a coroutine that works in slices across frames. Either way the per-scene result stays cached as it is now.

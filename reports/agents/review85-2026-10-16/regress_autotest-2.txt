[minor/low] src/Core/AutoTest.cs:1259  Steady-hand thresholds are per frame, so the 12 fps virtual headset flags a still gun
  what:   `bool shaky = maxA > 0.2f || maxM > 0.002f;` (SteadyCheck's Measure). The samples in `Sample` (line 1266, WaitForEndOfFrame at line 1274) are one frame apart, so each value is a per-frame delta. The file's own comment at line 1550 says the null driver runs at 12 frames a second, and tools/vrshots.py runs the autotest under that null driver.
  why:    At 12 fps the same motion between samples is about 7 times larger than at 90 fps, so residual motion of a still gun can read as SHAKES (a Problem line), and the `held still ... between frames` metrics change with frame rate. Runs at different frame rates cannot be compared, and improve rounds can chase a fake regression.
  change: Measure per second: divide each delta by `Time.unscaledDeltaTime` at the sample and compare deg/s and mm/s to the thresholds, or sample on a fixed time grid (`WaitForSecondsRealtime(0.05f)`). Print the sample rate in the Results line.

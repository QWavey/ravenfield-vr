[minor/medium] src/Core/PerfProbe.cs:53  Per-section byte counts include worker threads' allocations
  what:   Begin and End read GC.GetTotalMemory(false) (lines 38 and 53) and attribute the difference to the section.   why:    The 'bytes allocated per call' column (line 168) includes whatever any thread allocated in the window, so a main-thread section that allocates nothing can show kilobytes per call.

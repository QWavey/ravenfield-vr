[major/medium] src/Core/AutoTest.cs:2250  Gun-into-wall test puts the wall at a fixed 0.75 m, which for rifles can sit inside the gun
  what:   'wall.transform.SetPositionAndRotation(head.position + flat * 0.75f - Vector3.up * 0.3f, ...)' (2250, scale 1.2 x 1.2 x 0.15 at 2251) and the hand is pushed 'start.position + flat * (0.5f * i / 30f)' (2262) for every weapon. The muzzle is read at 2270 ('held.Rig.ToWorld(held.Rig.Data.MuzzlePoint)') but never used to place the wall.
  why:    The wall's near face is at 0.675 m. A rifle's muzzle is typically about 0.5-0.7 m beyond its grip (estimate), and the hand rests roughly 0.35-0.5 m in front of the head (2315 uses 0.58 m as arm reach). [minor/medium] src/Core/AutoTest.cs:2320  Scripted throw moves the hand at about 1 m/s but reports 2.5 m/s; pass thresholds are unexplained
  what:   'Vector3 swing = ...normalized * 2.5f' (2320) with 'start.position + swing * (0.011f * i)' (2324) and 'ScriptedVelocity = swing' (2325); the pass test is 'speed < 1.2f' (2358). The pouch throw uses 3 m/s (2617) with 'swing * (0.011f * i)' (2621) and 'flown > 0.6f' (2646). VRHand.cs:124 takes the scripted velocity as the throw velocity.
  why:    Each frame the hand advances 0.011 m, about 1.0 m/s at 90 Hz, but the throw launches at ScriptedVelocity (2.5 m/s). The on-screen swing and the launch speed disagree, and both depend on frame rate. The 1.2 m/s and 0.6 m thresholds are not derived from the swing, so a slower or faster swing changes the result.
  change: Advance the offset by swing * Time.deltaTime (accumulated) instead of 0.011f * i, so motion and ScriptedVelocity agree at any frame rate. Derive the pass thresholds from the swing speed (for example 0.5 * swing.magnitude for the drop, and a fraction of speed * 0.5 s for the pouch flight).

  what:   's.StartBytes = GC.GetTotalMemory(false);' (38) and 'long bytes = GC.GetTotalMemory(false) - s.StartBytes;' (53). GetTotalMemory is process-wide.
  why:    The mod runs work off the main thread while sections are open: GunVolume builds on the ThreadPool (src/Weapons/GunVolume.cs:583), LensEffects runs its own band threads (src/Rig/LensEffects.cs:202-208), and EyeFilm has a writer thread (src/Core/EyeFilm.cs:57).

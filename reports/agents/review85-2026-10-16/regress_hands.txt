[minor/medium] src/Rig/HandModel.cs:1178  Free-hand fist toggle restarts the 0.3 s pose fade and slows the fingers
  what:   TargetCurls sets `_pose = HandPoses.Get(HandPoseId.Fist)` for Free hands without a skeleton when grip > 0.9 and trigger > 0.9 (HandModel.cs:1178-1180). Apply (HandModel.cs:657-664) treats every `_pose.Id` change as a new pose: `_poseChangedAt = Time.unscaledTime`, then `Mathf.Lerp(7f, 25f, fade)` for the curl rate and `Mathf.Lerp(9f, 40f, fade)` for the joint rate.
  why:    Each crossing of the 0.9 threshold restarts the slow ramp. Integrating the rates, 50 ms after a change a finger has covered about 35% of its move (was about 71%) and its joints about 44% (was about 86%). With grip or trigger hovering near 0.9 the pose flips every frame, the fade never ends, and the fingers ease at 7/s and 9/s the whole time. Only controllers without a finger skeleton (VRInput.HasSkeleton false) are affected.
  change: Run the fade only on the base pose from HandPoses.For (the id before the Fist override): keep a `_fadeBase` compared against the pre-override id, or skip the fade when the change is only the Fist override. Optionally add hysteresis to the Fist override (enter above 0.9, leave below 0.8).

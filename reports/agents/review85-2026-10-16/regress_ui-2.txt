[major/high] src/Weapons/WeaponDrops.cs:841  Swapped-away weapons never expire and bypass the drop cap
  what:   Commit b0a99c2 (0df7b82 area) sets swapped.Keep = true at line 786 ("your rifle, swapped ... was deleted 90 s later"). Cull() then skips them: line 841 'if (expired && !d.Keep) Remove(d);' and the cap loops at lines 850 and 854 count and evict only 'OwnerSlot < 0 && !Keep'. Swapped drops come from Spawn() (line 170), which never sets OwnerSlot, so it stays -1. Keep is the only protection, and it now exempts the drop from both lifetime and cap.
  why:    Every time the player swaps a weapon for one lying on the ground, the weapon put down stays in _drops forever (no DroppedWeaponLifetime, not counted toward MaxDroppedWeapons, default 16). A long session with many swaps and no re-pickup grows _drops without bound; each keeps its colliders, rigidbody and baked OwnedMeshes, and the nearest-drop scan visits every drop's colliders each frame (its own comment says 'every collider of every drop, for both hands, every frame'). Before b0a99c2 these were deleted after the lifetime.
  change: Keep the Keep flag from the lifetime, but count Keep drops toward max: in the cap loop (line 852) evict non-Keep drops first (oldest first), then the oldest Keep drop as a last resort; or give Keep drops their own longer timeout (e.g. 10x DroppedWeaponLifetime) and include them in the count. Note: this file is outside the assigned list; it was found while tracing HolsterDisplay/PickUp callers.

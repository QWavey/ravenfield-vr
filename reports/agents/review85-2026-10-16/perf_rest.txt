[major/high] src/Weapons/VRScope.cs:252  Scope lens renders a full extra scene frame while its picture is blacked out
  what:   Line 252: `bool active = aiming || dist < 0.22f;`. Line 270 `_camera.enabled = true;` runs before the eye-offset math at lines 275-283, which ends with `float brightness = Mathf.Clamp01(1f - (offAxis - 0.03f) / 0.04f); _lensMaterial.color = new Color(brightness, brightness, brightness, 1f);`. Brightness is 0 from offAxis = 0.07 m. The shadow disc covers the whole lens at about 0.065 m (push = (offAxis - 0.008)/0.03 reaches 1.91 lens radii, the edge of the clear part; the code comment says 'all of it by about 6.5 cm'). The lens quad uses Sprites/Default, so its colour multiplies the render-texture output: at 0 the camera's picture is invisible.
  why:    Whenever the weapon is within 22 cm of the head or aimed, and the aiming eye is more than about 6.5 cm off the sight line (gun raised to the face but not settled, or the head offset), the mod still renders the whole scene a second time every frame into a ScopeResolution texture (default 768 px, antiAliasing 2, VRConfig.cs:247) and nothing it draws is visible. The camera also copies the VR camera's clearFlags, allowHDR and depthTextureMode (lines 265-269), so any depth pre-pass the VR camera uses is paid again. Unmeasured estimate: a second full culled pass plus any shadow work (Unity normally builds shadow maps per camera) is plausibly 0.5-2 ms per frame of the 11 ms budget, only in the off-line state.
  change: Move the offAxis / brightness computation (lines 275-282) above line 270. When `brightness <= 0f`, keep `_camera.enabled = false`, set `_lensMaterial.color` to black, still call SetShadow, and return. The camera re-enables on the first frame the eye is back within about 6.5 cm; it renders that frame immediately, so the stale texture never shows.

[major/high] src/Weapons/HeldWeapon.cs:844  Aim-down-sights engage and release give no haptic or sound
  what:   UpdateAim decides the aim state with hysteresis (lines 824 and 842) and stores it at line 844 (`Aiming = aim;`). AimingPatch (WeaponPatches.cs:272) and GameInputBridge read it. No Haptic or Sounds.Play call is tied to the change. The haptics in src/Weapons cover shots, reload handling, holsters, grabs and wall knocks, and none fires when the sight comes up.
  why:    In VR the player brings the stock to the eye and the sight picture locks or drops with no tick. Nothing tells them the eye is on the line; they have to look through the lens to know. Aiming is the most repeated action in a fight, so every shot is affected.
  change: After `Aiming = aim;` (HeldWeapon.cs:844), when the value changed and the last change was more than about 0.15 s ago, call `_system.Rig.Dominant.Haptic(0.02f, 160f, 0.35f)` on engage and a lighter pulse (about 0.015 s, 140 Hz, 0.25) on release, and play a short sight click through a new MechSound entry (see the fire-mode finding). The cooldown stops buzzing when the eye hovers at the threshold.

[major/high] src/Weapons/WeaponSystem.cs:494  Fire-mode toggle plays the empty-trigger click
  what:   HandleFireModePress plays `Sounds.Play(MechSound.DryFire, Rig.Dominant.Palm.position, 0.5f);` (line 494) after a SEMI/AUTO toggle. That is the same clip as the empty-gun click at line 447, and the mode name is shown only as text through FlashMessage (line 493). The DryFire clip is a 60 ms click plus a 2600 Hz ring (ProceduralSounds.cs:33-34).
  why:    A player who switches to semi hears a dead trigger and may reach for a magazine that is not empty. Telling 'switched' from 'empty' then depends on a small text readout in the gun that a VR player may not be looking at.
  change: Add `Selector` to the MechSound enum (ProceduralSounds.cs:7) and build it next to DryFire (ProceduralSounds.cs:33): two short clicks about 25 ms apart, lower for SEMI and higher for AUTO. Play it at line 494 instead of DryFire. Also strengthen the empty-gun haptic at WeaponSystem.cs:448, which is 0.01 s at amplitude 0.25 and the weakest pulse in the file, to about 0.02 s at 0.4.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/Casings.cs:66  Ejected case speed and spin are the same for every gun
  what:   Eject sets `velocity = hand + right * Random.Range(1.8f, 2.6f) + up * Random.Range(0.8f, 1.5f) + back * Random.Range(0.1f, 0.5f)` (line 66) and spin `Random.insideUnitSphere * 25f` (line 68) for every kind. Kind changes only the mesh (line 129) and, for shells, the mass (line 136: `kind == Kind.Shell ? 0.03f : 0.012f`).
  why:    A 9 mm pistol case and a rifle or LMG case leave the port with the same speed range and the same tumble, so the pistol's brass flies as far and spins as hard as a rifle's. Players who handle these guns notice the generic behaviour.
  change: Give each Kind (Pistol, Rifle, Heavy, Shell) its own speed and spin factor, for example Pistol 0.7x, Rifle 1.0x, Heavy 1.25x, Shell 0.8x with a heavier, slower tumble, applied to the two Random ranges and the spin at lines 66-68. Keep the hand-velocity term. The small table can sit next to the meshes in EnsureAssets. The numbers are a starting point to tune by eye.

[minor/high] src/Weapons/WeaponOverrides.cs:65  Edits to the override file never reach weapons already analysed
  what:   The file-change check (GetLastWriteTimeUtc and ClearCache, lines 65-79) lives in EnsureLoaded, which only Apply calls (line 109). Apply runs only on a WeaponAnalyzer.Get cache miss (WeaponAnalyzer.cs:108-111 return early; Apply at :123). The warm-up analyses every installed weapon in the menu (WeaponWarmup.cs:148), and RigFor keeps each WeaponRig in `_rigs` until a scene change (WeaponSystem.cs:340-348, cleared at 805). After the warm-up nothing calls EnsureLoaded. The comment at lines 75-76 ('weapons analysed already are analysed again (from their next draw)') does not happen.
  why:    A tuner who edits RavenfieldVR.weapons.json while the game runs sees no effect until a restart or until a new weapon loads. The documented behaviour is not what the code does.
  change: Expose the stamp check as `WeaponOverrides.Refresh()` returning whether it changed, and call it at the top of WeaponSystem.AttachHeld (WeaponSystem.cs:297; Held is null there). When it returns true, clear `_rigs` so the next draw rebuilds the rig through WeaponAnalyzer.Get. The first draw of each edited weapon then pays one analysis hitch, which is the intended effect.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/WeaponOverrides.cs:138  Magazine override changes the path but keeps the detected magazine parts
  what:   The `magazine` branch sets only `d.MagazinePath` (line 143). MagazinePartPaths (WeaponRig.cs:79), MagazineIsSkinned (WeaponRig.cs:81) and MagazineToggles (WeaponRig.cs:83) keep the values detected for the original magazine: parts that move 'together' with it (WeaponAnalyzer.cs:2725) and the toggle and skinned flags (WeaponAnalyzer.cs:2075-2076).
  why:    With an override that names a different transform, the rounds, follower or base plate detected for the old magazine stay tied to the wrong piece, and a skinned-bone flag can make the mod move the wrong object when the magazine is drawn. The player sees parts left in the gun or moving with the wrong part.
  change: When the magazine override resolves, re-run the magazine-part detection for the new transform (the pass around WeaponAnalyzer.cs:2690-2729), or at minimum clear MagazinePartPaths and reset MagazineIsSkinned and MagazineToggles, so no part of the other magazine is carried over.

[minor/low] src/Weapons/WeaponOverrides.cs:170  Pitch override turns the aim axis but not the sight line
  what:   The pitch block (lines 170-175) rotates AimAxis and Up only. EyeDirection (WeaponRig.cs:110) and each SightLine.EyeDirection (WeaponRig.cs:47) keep their pre-override values. HeldWeapon.cs:811 uses the sight line's EyeDirection for the eye check, and the analysis set those from the bore (WeaponAnalyzer.cs:880, 1020).
  why:    With pitch set, the sight line and the barrel direction disagree by the pitch angle, so a lined-up eye no longer points down the bore the mod now uses. Whether this is a bug depends on intent: the doc on WeaponOverride.pitch (WeaponOverrides.cs:27-28) says 'barrel pitch', which could mean bore-only.
  change: If pitch should move the whole gun, apply the same rotation q to d.EyeDirection and to every d.Sights[i].EyeDirection in that block. If it is bore-only, say so in the WeaponOverride.pitch doc comment and leave the code as it is.

[minor/medium] src/Weapons/WeaponWarmup.cs:171  Warm-up pacing cap breaks the 6 ms budget for long analyses
  what:   `int rest = Mathf.Clamp(Mathf.CeilToInt((float)watch.Elapsed.TotalMilliseconds / 6f), 1, 45);`. The header (lines 13 and 170) promises about 6 ms per frame on average. Once an analysis takes more than about 270 ms, the rest is capped at 45 frames, so the cost per frame grows with the analysis time.
  why:    An analysis of 600 ms (the comment says detailed models are slow) is followed by 45 idle frames, which is about 13 ms per frame over that span: above the roughly 11 ms budget of a 90 Hz headset. The analysis itself is one unbroken frame (lines 148-149 call WeaponAnalyzer.Get and PrefitShape with no yield), so a very large model is one long hitch in the menu either way.
  change: Remove the upper bound so the rest is `Mathf.Max(1, Mathf.CeilToInt(ms / 6f))`, which keeps the average at the promised 6 ms. Keep the one-frame cost in mind: it cannot be paused inside WeaponAnalyzer.Get or PrefitShape.

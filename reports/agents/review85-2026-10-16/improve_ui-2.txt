[major/high] src/UI/VRSettingsPanel.cs:50  Settings panel is about 2.2 m tall, so header and footer sit 36-42 degrees off the view centre
  what:   `rect.sizeDelta = new Vector2(CanvasWidth, _height + 36f);` with `_height` ending at about 2230 px (31 rows at 58 px plus headers, Build() lines 57-109) and `_root.transform.localScale = Vector3.one * (0.95f / CanvasWidth)` (line 315) make the panel roughly 2266 x 0.00097 m = 2.2 m tall. It is placed at `MenuDistance * 0.85` = 1.275 m (line 303) with its centre at head height minus 0.1 m. The comment at line 192 still says 'about 1.5 m tall'. Nothing scrolls or pages.
  why:    Measured from the centre of view, the header is about 36 degrees up and the Close / Mod keys / Recenter / SteamVR bindings footer about 42 degrees down. Those are well outside a comfortable gaze range (about +/-20-25 degrees), so reading or pressing the top or bottom rows means tilting the head every time, and the most used buttons are the farthest away.
  change: Split Comfort+Weapons and Display into two pages with a page toggle in the header, and move Close, Recenter and Mod keys into the header row. Alternatively make the content scroll with the thumbstick. Aim for a total height near 1.1 m (about 1150 px at this scale) and update the stale comment at line 192.

[minor/medium] src/UI/SquadPointing.cs:94  Pointing has no hysteresis, so a hand drifting past 32 degrees on release cancels the order
  what:   `float bend = Vector3.Angle(h.Pointer.Forward, fromEye); if (bend > Straight) continue;` with `Straight = 32f` (line 30). `Pointing` and `PointingHand` are reset every frame (lines 72-73). On release, the same frame's state decides: the IssueSquadPointOrder prefix (lines 315-318) calls `CancelPointSquadOrder()` when `!SquadPointing.Pointing`.
  why:    A single frame over 32 degrees at the moment the squad button is let go throws the order away. The label also swaps between 'GO TO' and 'POINT WITH A HAND' on hand tremor. The game plays a cancel sound (FpsActorController.cs:1704), so the player hears a cancel they did not intend.
  change: Remember the previous state before the reset: `bool was = Pointing; VRHand prev = PointingHand;`. Then use `float limit = was && prev != null ? Straight + 10f : Straight;` and `if (bend > limit) continue;`, preferring `prev` when both hands qualify.

[minor/high] src/UI/VRSettingsPanel.cs:230  Stepper buttons step once per press, so throw strength from 1.0x to 4.0x needs 30 trigger presses
  what:   `MakeButton(row, "+", 34f, 56f, () => step(1), out _);` (line 230), and the same for the minus button at line 237: one step per click, no repeat while held. Step sizes are 0.1 for throw strength (lines 89-90) and render scale (line 95), and 0.25 for recoil and lens flash (lines 86, 102-103).
  why:    In VR each step is a separate trigger pull at a 56 px target, so long ranges are tiring and easy to overshoot. The stepper's values also go through BepInEx config, which saves on every set (the note at AutoTest.cs:464 says so), so a naive hold-repeat would rewrite the file many times a second.
  change: Repeat while held: step on press, then after about 0.4 s repeat about every 0.1 s until release (an EventTrigger PointerDown/PointerUp on the button, or an Update-polled hold timer). While a hold is in progress, turn off ConfigFile.SaveOnConfigSet and save once on release.

[minor/high] src/UI/WristHud.cs:150  Health colours use fixed 60/30 HP, but the Katana rage raises max health to 150
  what:   `float hp = Mathf.Max(0f, actor.health);` (line 135) and `_text.color = hp > 60f ? light : hp > 30f ? amber : red;` (line 150). The text is `hpWhole + " HP"` (line 146) with no max, and the cache key at lines 140-145 ignores max. The game's Katana sets `rageMaxHeatlhMultiplier = 1.5f` (Katana.cs:137) and `player.maxHealth = defaultMaxHealth * rageMaxHeatlhMultiplier` (Katana.cs:328). The game's own bar divides by max (IngameUI.cs:693).
  why:    At a max of 150, the healthy colour holds down to 61 HP (about 41%) and amber only starts below 60 HP (40%). Red appears at 30 HP (20%). On the normal 100 max those colours appear at 60% and 30%. A player at 90 of 150 HP, which is 60%, still sees the healthy colour.
  change: Compute `float frac = actor.health / Mathf.Max(1f, actor.maxHealth);` and use 0.6 and 0.3 as the thresholds. Show `hpWhole + " / " + Mathf.RoundToInt(actor.maxHealth) + " HP"`, and add the max to the cache check at line 140 so a max change refreshes the text.

  what:   `Vector3 centre = s.Go.transform.TransformPoint(s.Centre); bool visible = Vector3.Distance(centre, head.position) > 0.2f + s.Length * 0.25f || Vector3.Dot(head.forward, (centre - head.position).normalized) < 0.2f;` (lines 69-71). `s.Length` is the full barrel length (line 137), so the barrel's ends lie up to `Length * 0.5` from the centre.

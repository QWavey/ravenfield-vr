[major/medium] src/Rig/HandModel.cs:696  No haptic when the fingers close on the held weapon
  what:   In Apply the closing branch calls `SolveFinger(f, _curl[f]);` (line 696). SolveFinger stops a joint on the model and sets `_touched` (line 1019), but nothing in HandModel.cs calls `_hand.Haptic` (grep finds no haptic call in src/Rig/HandModel.cs). Other systems pulse on their own events: WeaponSystem.cs:214 on a magazine grab, WeaponSystem.cs:613 on a holster.
  why:    The grab pulse fires when the grip is pressed, but nothing fires when the fingers and palm actually reach the grip. Wrapping a pistol or rifle grip is silent, so the player cannot feel where the hand has landed on it.
  change: Add `private readonly bool[] _fingerTouch = new bool[5];` beside `_fingerSolved` (lines 721-723). Right after `SolveFinger(f, _curl[f])` at line 696, read `bool t = _touched;` (SlideOnGrip at line 883 also writes it, so read it immediately). On a rising edge (`t && !_fingerTouch[f]`) call `_hand.Haptic(0.012f, 160f, 0.2f)`, about the size of the finger taps at ReloadInteractions.cs:720. Store `_fingerTouch[f]` with `_fingerSolved` and restore it on the reuse path so the pulse does not repeat. For the palm, add the same rising-edge test on `PalmTouches(n, 0f)` in SettlePalm (lines 814-826).

[minor/low] src/Rig/HandModel.cs:540  Glove looks live while its controller is lost
  what:   VRHand.UpdatePose keeps the last tracked pose when the controller drops out: `else if (_hasLastRaw) { rawLocal = _lastRawLocal; Tracked = false; }` (VRHand.cs:82-86). HandModel.Apply never reads `_hand.Tracked`; the only reads in the hands area are in ArmIK.cs:85.
  why:    While the controller is out of view the hand moves with the body and holds its last finger pose, drawn exactly like a tracked hand. The player cannot tell that the hand has frozen, and a held gun keeps its grip with no sign of the loss.
  change: In Apply, record when `!_hand.Tracked` began and, after about 0.3 s, dim the glove toward grey. Use a per-renderer MaterialPropertyBlock colour multiplier, not the shared material: HandShape.Material is shared by both hands (HandModel.SetGloveColor, lines 373-380), so changing it would dim both gloves.

[minor/medium] src/Rig/HandModel.cs:581  Hidden glove freezes its fingers, so they pop back on reshow
  what:   `if (Suppressed || HiddenByHeld) { _graspOffset = Vector3.zero; return; }` returns before the thumb base (line 680), the palm push (650-656) and the finger loop (681-739). So `_curl`, `_joint`, `_thumbBase` and `_push` stop updating while the glove is hidden.
  why:    With the weapon's own arms shown, the glove is suppressed (HeldWeapon.cs:1157-1161). When it comes back, the palm snaps to the tracked hand at once (`_root.SetPositionAndRotation`, line 612), but the fingers ease from the pose they had before hiding (k 7-25/s, joints 9-40/s). The player sees a palm already in place with fingers still closing or opening.
  change: Stop returning early for the finger loop. Keep only the root placement and the grasp-offset reset skipped while hidden (the renderer is already disabled by SetVisible), and let lines 647-741 run every frame. Nothing is drawn while hidden, so there is no visual cost.

[minor/low] src/Rig/HandModel.cs:1276  Fixed finger cap ignores grip size for unmeasured weapons
  what:   `float cap = HoldCap(_hand.Role);` then fingers 2-4 are clamped to it (lines 1276-1280). HoldCap (lines 1288-1296) returns 0.85 for Weapon, Magazine, Throwable and Cockpit and 0.75 for Foregrip. The comment at 1274-1275 says these leave about 3 cm of a pistol grip and 4 cm of a handguard.
  why:    The cap is used only when there is no measured solid (`Closing` is false, line 1256). Every such weapon then closes to the same curl whatever its grip diameter. A thick grip is passed through by the fingers, and a slim handle is left with a gap of several centimetres.
  change: Take the cap from the weapon when its grip width is known: add a `public float GripCap = -1f;` set by HeldWeapon from the measured grip, and use `float cap = GripCap >= 0f ? GripCap : HoldCap(_hand.Role);` at line 1276. Keep HoldCap as the fallback for weapons with nothing measured.

[major/high] src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:1043  A magazine, round or shell pushed into a well that refuses it gets no feedback
  what:   `if (!AcceptsNow(prop)) { prop.OnRail = false; if (_pullingIn == prop) _pullingIn = null; return; }` (lines 1043-1051) has no haptic, sound or flash. Refusals come from AcceptsNow (1005-1020: other weapon's WeaponKey, a magazine while Ammo.MagInserted, a rocket while MagRounds > 0, a shell when full or breech closed) and from TryMagKick's `if (!Data.MagKickable) return false;` (1258-1261), which turns a second magazine pushed into a deep-well pistol into a plain pass-through.
  why:    The player drives a magazine into the well, it slides past the mouth and nothing happens. They cannot tell whether the gun is full, the magazine is the wrong one, or the grab simply missed. The only cue for an accepted magazine is the lip sound and haptic at 1087-1091, so a refusal feels like a dropped input.
  change: In the `!AcceptsNow` branch at 1043, when the prop is inside the well window (same geometry as LinedUpUnderWell at 1185-1194 but without its AcceptsNow gate: along > -0.03, lateral < 0.06, angle < 40) and a throttle has passed (a new `_refusedAt` float, about 1 s), call `off.Haptic(0.03f, 90f, 0.35f)` and `_held.FlashCounter(reason)`. Reason: "WRONG MAG" when prop.WeaponKey != Data.Key, "FULL" for rocket or shell (same text as line 975), "LOADED" for a magazine when Ammo.MagInserted.

[major/medium] src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:1078  Loose rocket lock draws it from the wrong end, shifting it by its length or flipping it
  what:   On a loose lock: `prop.RailHandOffset = along - looseDepth;` (1078) then `along = looseDepth;` (1080) and `prop.RailDepth = Mathf.Clamp(along, 0f, prop.RailLength);` (1085). looseDepth is the depth of whichever end is nearer the mouth (LooselyLined, 1156-1168). But RailPose (MagazineProp.cs:1495-1499) always places TipLocal at RailDepth with seat.rotation, so the drawn rocket is always tip-in.
  why:    A rocket held base-first, with its far end deeper, is drawn with its tip at the base's depth, so it sits back from the hand by about its own length (RailHandOffset = length times cos of its tilt). ShowOnRail (ReloadInteractions.cs:1700-1705 and 1220-1239) then re-seats the glove onto the drawn rocket, so the hand jumps. A rocket held reversed (near end is the tip, axis about 180 degrees from the insert; `Mathf.Min(angle, 180f - angle)` at 1166 accepts it) is drawn turned 180 degrees on lock. ShowAt's 0.08 s blend (MagazineProp.cs:94-132) softens the snap but not the offset or the flip. This is the rocket-orientation path the user asked about.
  change: Make the drawn pose match the hand on loose locks. Add a per-prop flag (e.g. `RailReversed`) set at the loose lock (1078-1085) when the near end is the base or the axis is reversed. Have MagazineProp.RailPose (1495-1499) rotate the seat by 180 degrees about an axis perpendicular to InsertDirLocal and measure depth from BaseLocal, so the end the hand holds is the end drawn at the mouth. Do not simply substitute the near-end depth into RailDepth without that, because RailPose draws the tip there.

[minor/low] src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:1070  Lock-on tolerances are fixed per kind and ignore prop size, unlike the kick reach
  what:   `bool lined = lateral < (grip ? 0.045f : rocket ? 0.06f : 0.035f) && along > -0.03f ...` (1070-1071), `lateral > 0.07f + prop.RailLateral0` (1111) and `lateral < 0.06f` in LinedUpUnderWell (1193) are constants. The kick reach uses `prop.CrossHalf` (1244-1245), and MagazineProp has size classes BoxSized (MagazineProp.cs:74) and PistolSized (66).
  why:    A box or drum magazine (CrossHalf > 0.055) must have its centre within the same 3.5 cm of the well axis as a rifle magazine, though it is much wider, so starting one is relatively harder and the feel differs by magazine size. The kick already scales with size, so the lock is inconsistent with it.
  change: Derive the lock tolerance from prop.CrossHalf, for example `float lockReach = Mathf.Clamp(prop.CrossHalf + 0.015f, 0.035f, 0.07f);`, use it at 1070 and 1193 and set the leave-rail limit at 1111 to about twice that. Check the real CrossHalf values (MagazineProp.cs:171, 286) before tuning, since these numbers are estimates.

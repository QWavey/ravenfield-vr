[major/high] src/Weapons/WeaponAnalyzer.cs:38  Caliber in a magazine name (9x19, 7.62x39) makes it an optic
  what:   NotMagName (lines 35-39) ends with the alternative (^|[^a-z0-9])\d+(\.\d+)?x($|[^a-z]), meant for magnifiers such as 'Mag 2x'. IsMagName (line 41) is MagName.IsMatch(name) && !NotMagName.IsMatch(name). The reload candidate scan at line 2113 also does '|| NotMagName.IsMatch(t.name)' and drops the part outright.
  why:    In 'Mag_9x19', 'Magazine 7.62x39' or 'Mag_5.56x45' the 'x' is followed by a digit, which [^a-z] accepts, so the name is classed as optics. IsMagName is then false: FindMagByName (line 3083) skips the part, the name tie-break at lines 1953 and 2013 drops from 3x to 0.3x, and the reload-motion search at line 2113 skips it entirely. The gun then gets no magazine, or the wrong part is taken, so the real magazine is not ejected in VR.
  change: Replace the 'x($|[^a-z])' alternative with 'x(?![a-z0-9])'. A '2x' followed by a space, end or '-' still counts as a magnifier, while '9x19', '7.62x39' and '5.56x45' do not. Leave the rest of NotMagName unchanged.

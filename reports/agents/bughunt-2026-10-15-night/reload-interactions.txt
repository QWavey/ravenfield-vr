1. [CONFIRMED] [minor] Charging-handle stroke clamped to 14 cm, drawn handle moves its full travel
   src/Weapons/ReloadInteractions.cs:1385
   what: StrokeLength clamps a separate charging handle's travel to [0.03, 0.14] m, and the hand's grip point (RackPointWorld, line 1390) and the stroke fraction (UpdateRack, lines 1456-1459) are both mapped onto that clamped length.

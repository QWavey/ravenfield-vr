The reuse test at HandModel.cs:612-620 keeps the stored curls when !moved and the curls are settled, so the gun hand's fingers keep their old contact point.

- Effect: the gun hand's fingers stay stopped at the slide's old position, leaving a gap as the slide moves away. Reference lines in the report (1465-1467, 1482) are stale; the current lines are 1573-1595.
- Fix idea: for the gun hand, subtract the part's rest volume as RackSolid does, or bump SolidVersion with SlideOffset at HeldWeapon.cs:1580.

MECHANICS
- Caveat: method bodies are IL2CPP stubs, so all ordering, timing and transitions below are inferred from names, enum order, and signatures. Verified facts are the enum members, field names and types, method signatures, and which class holds which field. Haptic callers and gun call sites are not visible in the dump.
- Two state enums on Gun, one for logic and one for animation. Gun.SlideStates is the logical slide position: RETURNING, RETURNED, PULLING, PULLED, LOCKED. Gun.AnimationStates is the clip being played: NONE, NEUTRAL, SLIDEPULL, SLIDEPULLREVERSED, SLIDERETURN, SLIDERETURNREVERSED, SLIDELOCKED, SLIDELOCKEDREVERSED. The REVERSED variants are most likely the same clips played backward via the isReversed flag on CoPlayAnimation (inferred).
- Inferred slide cycle: RETURNED (at rest) to PULLING (SlidePull, clip SLIDEPULL) to PULLED (CompleteSlidePull after the clip ends) to LOCKED (SlideLocked, clip SLIDELOCKED, when the magazine is empty or the slide is held back) to RETURNING (SlideRelease, clip SLIDERETURN or the reversed lock clip) to RETURNED (CompleteSlideReturn). ForceCompleteSlidePull is the escape hatch so a state cannot get stuck.
- Hand racking: SlideGrip, PumpSlideGrip and CylinderSlideGrip project the hand onto slideDirection and measure slideDistance against lastDistance, using SimpleTransform parentToSlide and initalTransformToRoot as the frame. They set slideIsPulled and isSlideReleased. While the grip is held, Gun.SlideGrabbed() sets _isSlideGrabbed and the grip scrubs the animator through Gun.UpdateSlidePercentage(float perc), with isManualSlideMovement true. SlideOverride() with overridePerc and isSlideOverride is an alternative path that forces the percentage. On release, SlideGrabbedReleased() and SlideRelease() let the clip finish the return.
- Interruption and reversal: CoPlayAnimation(state, isReversed, startPerc, duration, AnimationCurve curve) starts from the current percentage and eases with a curve. leftOverTime, lastCompletedAnimation and playAnimationStateCoroutine (stopped and replaced) carry the state so a reversed clip can begin mid-way without snapping (inferred).
- Trigger and fire modes: Gun.isTriggerPressed is the raw button; isTriggerPulled is a threshold past it. isTriggerPulledOnAttach and GunGrip.isTriggerDownOnAttach block accidental fire when the trigger is already down on grab. Fire() is virtual and calls OnFire(); CeaseFire() ends it. GunGrip.Fire(Hand) starts the CoFire(Hand) coroutine, which loops while held when isAutomatic is set. isManual is the semi-auto counterpart. Two bool flags select the mode, and GunOptionSelect's FIREMODE entry toggles them through canChangeFiremode. Rate fields are roundsPerMinute, roundsPerSecond and fireDuration; fireSpeedHash sets the animator speed.
- Per-shot cycle on GunGrip: FireCycleState EMPTY, CHAMBER, FIRE, EJECT, advanced by NextCycleState() and SetCycleState(); cycleStateCount is the step count. Inferred pipeline: chamber a round, fire it, eject the case, then return to EMPTY or the next CHAMBER. percForNextState: float gates advancing (inferred). GetNextBullet() pulls the next round from the socket, and EjectBullet(bool HasBullet, bool isSilent) handles the spent or empty case.
- Chambering and cartridge state: Gun.chamberedCartridge (BulletObject) with cartridgeState NONE, UNSPENT, SPENT. Inferred: a live round goes UNSPENT on chamber and SPENT after firing. EjectCartridge() throws the case out of shellSpawnTransform, oriented by shellOrientationTransform. PullCartridge() loads the next round during slide return (CompleteSlideReturn). chamberedBulletGameObject, chamberedCartridgeGameObject and nextShellGameObject are the visible rounds. isChargedOnStart means the gun starts with a round chambered. RequiresChargingHandle() and chargingHandleHelperRenderer suggest guns that need a charging-handle rack before the first shot (inferred). Note that DuckSeason/Cartridge.cs is a minigame cartridge and unrelated to the gun chamber.
- Hammer: HammerStates UNCOCKED and COCKED on Gun.hammerState and GunGrip.isHammerRaised. It is separate from the slide state; cocking on pull and release on fire is inferred.
- Slide lock and release: the slide locks when the magazine runs dry (slideLockDuration is the hold). Magazine insert and remove (Gun.OnMagazineInserted, OnMagazineRemoved, MagazineSocket events) likely call SlideRelease (inferred). isSlideReleaseOnCollision lets the slide drop when the gun hits something (inferred). GunSFX.SlideLock with slideLockFire and slideLockFireSlow plays the lock sound. MagReleaseToGrab.UpdateMagGripPriority(int ammoCount) changes mag-grab priority with ammo count.
- Magazine socket state machine: MagazineSocket.m_States and MagazineSocketReciever.m_States are both EMPTY, SWITCHING, EJECTING, HOVERING, INSERTED, SLIDING. On the Props side, insertion progress is a spring-like 0 to 1 value (m_MovingPerc and m_PercVelocity, with m_ActivePerc and m_ActivePercVel). It runs in two phases, MoveTowardsStartInsertTarget() then SlideTowardsEndInsertTarget(). The hand transform is handed off between SimpleTransform m_HandToInsertTarget and m_InsertTargetToHand. The magazine is carried by a ConfigurableJoint (m_MagazineJoint from m_JointConfig, created by CreateJoint and removed by DestroyJoint or CoSelfDestructJoint). It is latched by MagazinePlug (OnPlugLocked, OnPlugUnlocked) and CoWaitLockMagazine().
- Insert guards: the insert distance is compared as a squared value (m_SqInsertDistance). requireExit and _requireExit make the magazine leave the socket before it can latch again. _cooldown and m_MagazineGrabbedTime add timing guards. DisableInteraction and EnableInteraction gate grabbing.

MagReleaseToGrab tracks m_IsPrimaryGrabbed and primaryGrip, so the grab picks the primary hand by score rather than by nearest distance (inferred).
- Kick and recoil: none of these classes has a hand or camera recoil field. Methods are GunShot(), GunError(), DryFire(), SlideLock() and so on. Two output mixers (gunshotOutputMixer, interactionOutputMixer) separate gunshots from interaction sounds. _gunshotAp and _gunshotTimer suggest pooled audio with a throttle (inferred). The fireSlow and dryFireSlow variants are most likely for slowed time (inferred; a Chronos folder exists in the dump). MagazineReciever.grabClips holds the magazine grab sounds.
- Haptics: Haptor (root namespace) is a per-controller engine. HapStack {hap_Add, hap_Override, hap_Ignore, hap_Duck} sets how a new event mixes with one already playing. hapticking, noDoubleHaptic and otherHaptor stop both hands buzzing at once. Weapon events: Haptic_WepFire(kickForce), Haptic_WepMagInsert(), Haptic_WepChargeHandle(), Haptic_WepClang(). Interaction events: Haptic_Click(bool down), Haptic_Tap(), Haptic_Knock(), Haptic_Subtle(), Haptic_Sliding(), Haptic_SlideFriction(), Haptic_Hit(float amp), Haptic_SoftSin and Haptic_HardSin (gateCount, length). - In-gun UI: GunOptionSelect has SelectOptions NONE, FIREMODE, EJECT. It runs a cursor and a colour fade (CoFadeToColor, CoFade) and shows the panel with OnOptionsActive and OnOptionsDeactive.
- Multiplayer and UI hooks: BroadcastGunFiredEvent() with _hasFiredSinceLastBroadcast and _lastTimeEventWasBroadcasted (time-throttled), and _poolee: Poolee ties the gun to pooled networking. LESSONS
- Pistol magazine insert (the thumb-lets-go-then-push request): model the insert as one continuous 0 to 1 progress with a spring and damper, as Boneworks does with m_MovingPerc and m_PercVelocity. Blend the hand pose from the grab pose toward the push pose over that progress instead of switching at a threshold. Carry the pose as a transform that lerps, similar to the m_HandToInsertTarget and m_InsertTargetToHand handoff.
- Split the insert into two phases: first align to a start point at the magwell, then slide along the insert axis to the end point, then latch. Thumb release can be the phase boundary, with the first phase driven by grab state and the second by insert progress.
- Latch with a short lock step (a plug or lock object plus a short wait, like CoWaitLockMagazine) before the magazine counts as inserted. Compare insert distance as a squared value to keep it cheap.
- Prevent instant re-insert after a release: require the magazine to leave the socket zone first (requireExit) and add a short cooldown.
- Double-mag reload pose fades: keep one pose and drive a blend weight (the slideBlend idea in PumpSlideGrip) with an AnimationCurve and a duration, instead of swapping poses. Fade the old pose out while the new one fades in. CoPlayAnimation's curve, duration and startPerc parameters show the pattern.
- When a fade or slide animation is reversed mid-way, start the reverse from the current percentage and keep the remaining time (leftOverTime, startPerc, isReversed). That way it never snaps.
- Keep logical state separate from animation state, as SlideStates and AnimationStates do. Logic stays correct when an animation is interrupted or reversed, and you author one clip and reuse its reversed variant.
- Racking hysteresis: use distinct pulled and returned thresholds (pullAnimationPerc, returnAnimationPerc), let the hand override the animation while it is on the slide, and add a force-complete path so states cannot get stuck.
- Put the kick on the gun, not the hand: animate the slide with a timed coroutine against a joint (GunAnimationManager style) and feed the same kickForce value into haptics so visual kick and buzz stay in sync.
- Haptics: keep a named library of events (WepFire, WepMagInsert, WepChargeHandle, WepClang, Click down and up, Tap, Knock) with a stacking policy (Boneworks HapStack: Add, Override, Ignore, Duck). A fire buzz should override a grab tap, not stack on it. Add a guard so the two hands do not double-buzz.
- Sound: keep separate clips for normal and slowed time (fire and fireSlow, dryFire and dryFireSlow), a dedicated dry-fire or error sound for invalid actions, and a separate mixer for gunshots.
- Model the fire cycle as explicit steps (EMPTY, CHAMBER, FIRE, EJECT). Gating each step makes it easy to stop fire when the slide is locked or the chamber is empty.
- Trigger: separate the raw press from the pull threshold, and ignore fire when the trigger was already down on grab (isTriggerPulledOnAttach).
- Mag grab choice: score candidate grip points (ValidateGripScore(Hand, worldPoint)) instead of picking the nearest, and change grip priority with ammo count (UpdateMagGripPriority).
- In VR, put fire mode and eject options on an in-gun panel with a fading cursor (GunOptionSelect: FIREMODE, EJECT), not on button combos.

Boneworks study (read-only Haiku agent, 2026-10-16): Boneworks magazines and sockets: well alignment, insertion, ejection, pouch, haptics (decompiled class structure; method bodies are interop stubs, so logic is inferred from names, types, enums and coroutine locals)

MECHANICS
- Scope: only the decompiled unhollowed Assembly-CSharp classes were read. No mod sources were opened and the game was not launched. Every mechanic below is inferred from names, types, enums and coroutine locals, since method bodies are stubs.
- Well geometry: a Socket is a trigger zone (OnTriggerEnter/Stay/Exit) with influenceRadius, endDistance and cutoffDirection. Plugs are tracked in _hoveredPlugs, _plugStays and _plugExits and processed in FixedUpdate (inferred). Thresholds are squared: Plug._effectiveRadiusSqr, MagazineSocket.m_SqInsertDistance, AlignPlug._deadZoneDistanceSqr, and sqrDistance locals in the coroutines (inferred: avoids sqrt per frame).
- Insert axis: MagazineSocketReciever defines it with startInsertTarget and endInsertTarget (Transforms). Insertion progress is one scalar, m_MovingPerc, with a paired damped velocity m_PercVelocity (inferred: SmoothDamp-style, not a timed lerp). A second pair, m_ActivePerc and m_ActivePercVel, likely covers the active/hover state (inferred).
- Hand projection: m_HandTargetTransform, m_HandToInsertTarget and m_InsertTargetToHand are SimpleTransforms. They convert the hand pose into insert-target space, so hand motion along the axis drives progress (inferred).
- Two-phase insert: phase 1 aligns the plug rotation via a ConfigurableJoint (AlignPlug._joint, MagazineSocketReciever.m_MagazineJoint, m_JointConfig) using ConfigurableJointExtensions.SetTargetRotation toward the socket orientation. Phase 2 slides along the axis (MoveTowardsStartInsertTarget, SlideTowardsEndInsertTarget). AlignPlug flags _isEnterTransition, _isExitTransition and _isStageTwoAnimation mark the phase boundaries (inferred).
- Temporary joint: CreateJoint(Rigidbody) builds the joint and CoSelfDestructJoint(source, target, time) destroys it after a timer. DestroyJoint() is the explicit path (inferred: the joint only pulls the mag in and then goes away).
- State machine: MagazineSocket.m_State and MagazineSocketReciever.m_State use the same enum {EMPTY, SWITCHING, EJECTING, HOVERING, INSERTED, SLIDING}. Likely path is EMPTY to HOVERING (plug enters the trigger) to SLIDING (past the squared distance threshold) to INSERTED (locked), with EJECTING for removal and SWITCHING for swapping mag type (inferred).
- Seated trigger (inferred chain): AlignPlug.OnPlugInsertComplete() -> MagazineSocket.OnPlugLocked(Plug) -> Socket.Lock(Plug) (sets LockedPlug and IsLocked, fires onPlugLockDelegate) -> MagazineSocketReciever.OnMagazineLocked() and MakeStatic(). Then MagazineSocket.OnMagazineInserted fires to Gun.OnMagazineInserted, MagReleaseToGrab.OnMagazineInserted and PlayerInventory.RegisterMagazine. CoWaitLockMagazine is a coroutine that waits on a distance check before the lock (inferred).
- Proxy grip: once inserted, MagazineSocket.UpdateProxyGripState(Hand) and _isProxyGripState hand grabs to the socket's proxyGrip (Socket.proxyGrip, AlignPlug.proxyGrip). OnProxyGrab/OnProxyRelease handle the hand (inferred: avoids re-grabbing the mag mid-slide).
- Grip scoring and priority: MagazineReciever.ValidateGripScore(Hand, Vector3 worldPoint) returns a float per grip candidate. MagazineReciever.priority, UpdateMagGripPriority(bool setToOne) and UpdateMagGripPriority(int) on MagReleaseToGrab change which grip wins (inferred). disableMagazineGrab, DisableInteraction() and EnableInteraction() gate grabbing during animation.
- Ejection: MagazineSocket.EjectMagazine(Action callback) forwards to MagazineSocketReciever.CoEjectMagazine(callback). The coroutine moves the host from localEnd back toward localStart along endToStart with increasing speed and acceleration, stops on sqrDistance, then calls the callback. EjectMagForPool(Action) is the variant that returns the mag to a pool (inferred). MagazineRelease() and MakeDynamic() give physics back.
- Release to hand: MagReleaseToGrab (gun-side release button) with m_IsPrimaryGrabbed and m_IsMagazineInserted, plus MagazineSocketReciever.UpdateMagazineGrab(Hand) and OnMagazineGrabbed(Hand). m_WasMagazineGrabbed and m_MagazineGrabbedTime suggest a timed grab window (inferred). SetHoverEject() and m_IsHoverEject suggest a hover-to-eject state (inferred).
- Ammo model: a global pool in PlayerInventory keyed by Weight (LIGHT, MEDIUM, HEAVY) with AddAmmo, RemoveAmmo, DecrementAmmo and GetAmmo. Magazine.ClaimAmmo(PlayerInventory) with IsAmmoClaimed means a mag takes rounds from the pool on first use. Per-mag rounds are _ammoCount, with GetNextBullet, EmptyMagazine(subAmt) and onGetNextBulletDelegate for the gun (inferred). The top rounds are shown via firstBullet, secondBullet and CheckBulletArt.
- Compatibility: MagazineData.platform (Platform enum) is the likely gate against the gun platform. Gun.overrideMagazine can force a MagazineData (inferred).
- Pouch: AmmoPouch spawns mags from a SpawnGrip (OnSpawnGrab(Hand)). It cycles Platform through _platformStack (SwitchMagazine), shows per-platform art (MagazineArt[] with GameObject arrays, UpdateArt(Weight)), and shows light/medium/heavy counts on TextMeshPro. It reacts to hand slots via OnHandItemSlot and OnHandItemSlotRemoved. Inventory.RegisterSlot(name, slot) and the SubscribeTo/UnSubscribeFrom Insert and Remove helpers wire these by name.
- Reload variants: Gun has SetAutoReload(bool), AutoReloadWeapon(), PhantomLoad() and CoPhantomReload() (the coroutine has a local MagazinePlug, _plug_5__4). This suggests a scripted reload that takes the plug directly without the hand path (inferred). CheckGunRequirements and RequiresChargingHandle gate the slide when empty.
- Haptics: Haptor has per-event presets: Haptic_WepMagInsert(), Haptic_Click(bool down) for press and release, Haptic_Tap, Haptic_Knock, Haptic_Hit(float), Haptic_SlideFriction and Haptic_Sliding. Timed buzz uses co_HapticSine(gate, length, frequency, max_amplitude, min_amplitude) and Haptic_SoftSin/HardSin (gate count and length). SENDHAPTIC(delay, duration, frequency, amplitude) is the generic call. noDoubleHaptic suppresses duplicates.
- Sounds: GunSFX.magazineInsert and magazineDrop are AudioClip arrays, played by MagazineInsert() and MagazineDrop(). Grab sounds come from MagazineReciever.grabClips and AmmoPouch.grabClips.
- Timing: almost all motion is in IEnumerator coroutines (CoEjectMagazine, CoWaitLockMagazine, CoSelfDestructJoint, CoAutoGrab, ClipInteractionCooldown, CoPhantomReload, CoPlayAnimation). Timing is by frame yields with speed and acceleration, not animation clips (inferred from the coroutine locals).

LESSONS
- Drive the whole insert and eject from one progress scalar (0 to 1) along a start-to-end axis, and smooth it with a damped velocity instead of a timed lerp. This is the m_MovingPerc and m_PercVelocity pattern. A mid-slide reversal then stays continuous, which fits the slow release-to-push feel the user described.
- Project the hand pose onto the insert axis at grab time (the m_HandToInsertTarget idea) and convert hand motion along that axis into progress. A push gesture can then read as continuing the same slide, rather than as a separate control.
- Make the thumb-to-push hand-off a state change, not a boolean. Use explicit states (HOVERING, SLIDING, INSERTED, EJECTING, SWITCHING) and switch when the squared distance crosses a threshold, blending the old pose out over a short window instead of snapping.
- Split insertion into two stages with explicit enter and exit transition flags: stage one aligns the rotation, stage two slides on the axis. Blend the gesture weight between the stages. This is the fade-in and fade-out behaviour requested for the double mag reload.
- Add a dead zone near the seat (the _deadZoneDistanceSqr idea) so the mag does not jitter once it is almost home. Use squared distances for the checks to keep per-frame cost low.
- Eject as an accelerating reverse slide along the same axis, with a completion callback that hands the mag back to hand or physics only after the motion ends (CoEjectMagazine pattern).
- For a seat that finishes itself, use a temporary spring that exists only for a few seconds (CoSelfDestructJoint), so the mag is not left permanently physical.
- While the mag is sliding or inserted, lower its grip priority or redirect grabs to a proxy grip on the well, so the hand cannot re-grab it mid-motion.
- Use one haptic preset per event (insert, click on lock, tap on eject) plus one gated buzz during the slide. Keep separate press and release clicks (Haptic_Click(bool down)).
- For double mag reloads, run a scripted coroutine that takes over the plug (the CoPhantomReload pattern). Keep the current pose for the old mag, fade the new pose in, and use a SWITCHING state so the two poses never run together.

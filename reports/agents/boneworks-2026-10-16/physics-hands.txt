MECHANICS
- Scope: all logic is inferred from member names, types, enums and field lists. The IL2CPP method bodies are interop stubs, so no numeric spring, force or break values are visible. Only the decompiled game folder was read, not the mods.
- Hand driving (PhysHand): the hand is a Rigidbody (rb) pulled toward the tracked controller pose every physics step by UpdateHand(targetLocalPos, targetRotation, armlength, joint, shoulderWorld, bodyDominance, gripInput). Two paths exist, selected by forceViaJoint: a joint-drive path, and a direct path through ApplyForce/ApplyTorque. The joint argument is most likely Hand.joint (inferred).
- Force units are newtons: names such as newtonDamp, newtonsDamped, divByNewtons and newtonSecond suggest a PD-like controller with damping in newtons (inferred).
- Strength is asymmetric and frame-based: six per-direction limits (xPosForce, xNegForce, yPosForce, yNegForce, zPosForce, zNegForce) are clamped by MuscleClamp and MuscleMax, expressed in the _muscleSpace frame (inferred). Torque is capped by maxTorque and shaped by the TorqueMultiplier curve, and RelaxTorqueMult(grip) reduces it as the grip closes.
- Grip-dependent strength: Hand.SetGripStrength(perc) and PhysHand.gripMult scale force. SetFriction(grip) swaps between lowFriction and _highFriction PhysicMaterials (inferred).
- Safety layers: ReadSensors reads joint force and velocity. CheckStuck flags a blocked hand (_stuck). ClearJerk and _jerkedMult/angJerkMult reduce force after a sudden acceleration (inferred). Together these stop explosions and jitter.
- Forearm contact: forearmCol plus CollisionRelay and the _forearmCollision fields detect the arm hitting geometry. SLZ_Body.Arms exposes forearmLfColliding and collisionLfInfluence, which feed this back into the arm solve (inferred).
- Weight is felt through the object's RigidbodyData.mass, drag and inertia (InteractableHost._rbData), and through joint break limits. Grip.minBreakForce and maxBreakForce set the range, Hand.OnJointBreak handles a break, and SetGripStrength probably maps grip strength into that range (inferred). A heavy load therefore pulls the object out of the hand.
- Load read-back: JointConfiguration.currentForce and currentTorque are read-only joint reaction forces. PhysHand.ReadSensors(joint, ref divByNewtons) probably reads them as the load signal for weight and recoil (inferred).
- Body-side weight: PhysBody.totalMass is static and PhysBody.SetBodyMassDistribution splits mass across rbHead, rbPelvis, rbKnee and rbFeet. PhysGrounder.totalMass and _totalImpulse sum what the feet touch. PhysBody.footSupported and PhysHand.handSupported report how body weight is shared between ground and hands (inferred).
- Two-handed holding has explicit states: HandPhysState (Empty, RbOneHand, RbTwoHand, StaticOneHand, StaticTwoHand). RefreshHandStates(thisJ, otherJ) updates them from both hands' joints. InteractableHost.IsTwoHanded() and the onTwoHandAttachDelegate and onTwoHandDetachDelegate events announce the second hand (decoded from names).
- Two-hand pose merge: Grip.CalculateFinalOverride(current HandGripPair, other HandGripPair, root, ref final) blends both hands' grip targets into one final pose (inferred). Grip.bodyDominance is passed into UpdateHand and ApplyForce, so it probably decides how much load the dominant hand takes (inferred). Grip.GetSwingCircleConstraints and GetTwistConstraints limit hand rotation about the grip (inferred).
- Grip roles: Grip.Type is OVERRIDE, PRIMARY or SECONDARY. A gun has a triggerGrip (primary) and an otherGrips array (secondary). Grip.VirtualControllerModes (GENERIC, HANDGUN, RIFLE, SLIDE, SLIDELOOK) probably selects a different two-hand controller pose for rifle versus handgun (inferred).
- No dedicated stabiliser class exists. Searching Rig, Interaction and VRMK for stabil only hits unrelated classes (Enemy_Turret.stability, HoverPlate, FlightStabilizer). Two-handed steadiness probably emerges from two grip joints on one gun Rigidbody, a spring at each hand anchor, and the shared grip targets with swing and twist limits (inferred).
- Recoil: Gun.kickForce and GunGrip.kickForce are the recoil magnitude, applied when Gun.Fire, GunGrip.Fire or OnFire runs. MuzzleBreakVariables.RecoilCompensation and MuzzleRiseCompensation reduce it. The likely path is an impulse on the gun body, transmitted through the grip joints to each PhysHand (appliedForce, appliedTorque, _lastForce) (inferred).
- Recoil into the body: gun.shoulderPointTransform (and GunGrip's) plus PhysHand.shoulder and PrepShoulderMuscle(handVelocity, newtonsDamped) probably let the shoulder absorb the kick by damping hand velocity (inferred). If the impulse exceeds the grip's break force, Hand.OnJointBreak releases the gun (inferred).
- Throws and release: Grip.SaveHistory fills a velocity ring buffer, and GetAverageHistory(startDelayTime, sampleTime) averages it after a delay. That gives clean release velocity (decoded from names).
- Body and visuals: SLZ_Body is the physics skeleton (the 'mk' body). SLZ_BodyBlender.BlendPos and BlendRot blend the visual bones toward it. PhysicsRig tracks pelvisVelocity, pelvisAccel and headDelta (decoded from names).

LESSONS
- Make each hand a physical body pulled toward the tracked pose by a damped spring with a force cap. Grabs then collide and feel weighted, instead of teleporting or following the controller exactly.
- Use asymmetric per-direction force limits in a local frame. Pushing, pulling and lateral motion then feel different, which reads as a real arm.
- Scale hand force with grip input and swap friction with grip, rather than a binary attach. Light grips slip and hard grips hold.
- Give each held object a break force and map grip strength onto it. The object drops when the load exceeds the grip, and that gives weight feel for free.
- Read the constraint reaction force from the grip joint each step. Use it for weight feedback (haptics, arm sag) and for recoil feedback, instead of faking them.
- Track the two hands with explicit states (one-hand, two-hand, dynamic, static). Blend the two grip targets into one pose and give one hand a dominance weight. This avoids the two hands fighting over the same object.
- For a gun, apply recoil as an impulse on the gun body and let the grip joints transmit it. Let a shoulder anchor damp hand velocity, so the body absorbs the kick. A muscle or brake compensation value reduces it.
- Add anti-explosion safeguards: detect blocked hands and reduce force, and after a sudden acceleration (a jerk or a shot) reduce force for a short window.
- Keep a velocity history ring buffer for throws, and average it after a short delay so release does not spike.
- Store per-object joint presets and per-hand-pose overrides, and sleep the drives when the object is at rest to cut jitter and cost.

MECHANICS
- Hierarchy (explicit): Grip : MonoBehaviour. GenericGrip : Grip and TargetGrip : Grip. TargetGrip is the base of CylinderGrip, BoxGrip, BarrelGrip, SphereGrip, SlideGrip, PumpSlideGrip, GunGrip, MeshGrip, GravityGun and SightFlipGrip. CylinderGrip is the base of CylinderSlideGrip, CylinderFuseGrip and CylinderElectronicGrip. GunGrip is the base of HandGunGrip. UIInteractionVolume and SpawnGrip are other Grip-family classes. ForcePullGrip and PullDevice are standalone MonoBehaviours.
- Ownership (inferred): InteractableHost owns the object's Rigidbody and joints, its List<Grip> and List<ForcePullGrip>, and the HandGripPair list. Hand owns the hover search and the hold joint. Interactable sits between the two and provides the fallback score.
- Hover discovery (inferred from Hand.cs): each Hand runs an overlap sphere (static _hoverSphereRadius, m_OverlappingColliders sized by static m_ColliderArraySize, filtered by hoverLayerMask and m_IgnoredColliders). It resolves Interactable -> InteractableHost -> grips, tracks near hover in m_HoveringInteractable and far hover in m_FarHoveringInteractable, and can lock hover with HoverLock/HoverUnlock.
- Candidate choice (inferred): each candidate grip returns ValidateGripScore(hand, worldPoint) as a float. Interactable.defaulGripScore is the fallback, and Grip.priority breaks ties. Subclasses override scoring: GenericGrip uses a raycast from the hand (genericHandStates: isRaycastHit, raycastPoint, raycastNormal, backOfHandNormal); BoxGrip and BarrelGrip pick a zone with CheckZones(hand); MeshGrip uses GetRaycastHit.
- Hover state per hand per grip is a bitmask in HandToGripState.gripFlags: HOVER=1, FARHOVER=2, ATTACH=4, PULLING=8. Hover callbacks take an isOverride flag (inferred: lets a grip take over default hover handling, paired with Grip.Type OVERRIDE). Far-hover has a parallel OnFarHandHover* set.
- Free vs snapped placement: GenericGrip is free placement (the hand goes where the ray meets the surface). TargetGrip family is fixed placement: gripTargets[hand] holds a SimpleTransform that the hand targets, changeable with SetGripTargetTransform(hand, pos, rot). Pose is chosen by name (SetHandPose / GetHandPoseTarget string) and blends in within a per-zone radius (poseRadius, edgeHandPoseRadius, faceHandPoseRadius and so on, inferred).
- Attach / snatch (inferred from coroutine locals): Snatch(hand, isQuick) starts CoSnatch, which loops per fixed update (WaitForFixedUpdate, index i, handState, handPoseTarget name). It then runs CoMoveHandToTarget(hand, localPosition, localRotation), which eases the hand with progress perc, jerk, accel, pSpeed and rSpeed, and finalPosition and finalRotation. isQuick presumably skips the eased move. CoUpdateHandAnimations(hand) animates pose while attached. GripEvents.AttachedEvent fires on attach.
- Hold physics (inferred): the hand creates a ConfigurableJoint (CreateConfigurableJoint, UpdateAnchor) between the hand Rigidbody and the object body (InteractableHost._rb). Motion is set by JointConfiguration, per pose via ApplyConfiguration(handPoseName, hand, joint). Grip strength scales the drive via Hand.SetGripStrength(perc). Break thresholds come from Grip.minBreakForce / maxBreakForce (per zone in BoxGrip and BarrelGrip). OnJointBreak(breakForce) followed by CoJointBreakCooldown suggests a re-grab cooldown. Non-joint hold uses Hand.AttachedObject (isParentedToHand, originalParent).
- Sliding and rotating along a grip: (a) CylinderGrip: the contact point moves on a circle around the axis, GetPointOnPerimeter(radius, theta, limit, perc), where perc is position along the length and limit is an angle limit. Friction is curve-driven (PositionFrictionCurve, SweetGrabCurve around rotationalSweetSpot, RadiusCurve for tapered parts). fauxMovingGrip with fauxGripVelocity suggests a fake slide. UpdateHandOrientation(hand, force, torque) rotates the hand from applied force and torque. (b) TargetGrip rotation limits: rotationLimit, rotationPriorityBuffer and cosine variants applied as joint angular limits via GetSwingCircleConstraints, GetTwistConstraints and GetJointLimitsInWorld. (c) BoxGrip and BarrelGrip: discrete zones, each with its own pose, radius and break force, so the hand slides between zones via CheckZones. (d) Slide family: the hand follows a constrained slide axis (slideDirection, upDirection, slideDistance, lastDistance). isFlippable with flippedHandPose vs normalHandPose swaps the pose when flipped, and slideIsPulled and isSlideReleased track release. (e) SphereGrip: CalculateHandOrientation rolls the hand over the sphere.
- Two hands on one object: InteractableHost keeps HandGripPairs (hand + grip). FetchGrips(out primary, out secondary) -> bool and IsTwoHanded() gate two-hand logic. onTwoHandAttachDelegate and onTwoHandDetachDelegate fire on transitions. Grip.Type (PRIMARY, SECONDARY, OVERRIDE) assigns roles. Grip.CalculateFinalOverride(current, other, root, ref final) lets the primary grip compute the object pose from both hands; SlideGrip, PumpSlideGrip and GunGrip override it (inferred). InteractionOptions.MultipleHands and SwitchHands gate second-hand attach and role swap. bodyDominance weights body vs hand.
- Force pull (ForcePullGrip, inferred from fields and method names): far hover calls OnFarHandHoverBegin/Update/End on both the Grip and the ForcePullGrip. GripState moves IDELING -> LONGHOVERING -> PULLING, and LONGHOVERING suggests a dwell timer before pull is allowed. Pull(hand) starts CoPull, which uses a temporary joint (tempJoint) to draw the object to pullToHand, capped by maxForce and maxSpeed. absorbForceToBody sets how much the object's own body resists. perc tracks progress, and finger fields (startThumb through startPinky, fingerLimit) pre-curl the hand while the object flies in. On arrival, _isStartAttaching leads to OnStartAttach (a normal snatch), then OnForcePullComplete fires forcePullCompleteDelegate. CancelPull(hand) aborts. Gated by IsFarHoverDisabled / EnableFarHover and registered via AddForcePullGrip.
- Ranged gravity gun (GravityGun, inferred): selects PullPoint candidates from cone and sphere queries (pullPointsInCone, pullPointsInSphere, pullSphereRadius, maxDistance, pullableLayerMask) and then pulls, grabs, pushes or fires them. This is a separate system from ForcePullGrip.
- Release (inferred from Hand and InteractableHost): Grip.ForceDetach(isQuick) calls Hand.DetachJoint(isQuick, lastGrip), which calls CoDelayDestroyJoint(myJoint, isQuick, lastGrip). That coroutine has locals _wait, _drive (JointDrive), _time and _duration, which suggests the joint drive is eased out over a duration before the joint is destroyed, and isQuick skips the ease. GripEvents.DetachEvent fires on release. Throw velocity: Grip.SaveHistory(velocity, angVelocity) writes each frame into a ring buffer (velocityHistory, angVelocityHistory, velocityHistoryIndex). GetAverageHistory(startDelayTime, sampleTime, out angVelocity) averages a past window, which suggests the throw uses an averaged window rather than the last frame. isThrowable gates this.
- Magazine (MagReleaseToGrab, inferred): the magazine grip's priority depends on state. UpdateMagGripPriority(ammoCount) scales it, and m_IsMagazineInserted and m_IsPrimaryGrabbed track whether the mag is in the gun or is the primary grab. OnMagazineInserted, OnMagazineRemoved and OnGetNextBullet hook into MagazineSocket (magazineSlotReciever). So the mag becomes preferred only when out of the gun and scales with ammo count.
- Limitations: all method bodies are IL2CPP stubs, so no numeric values, curve shapes or branch logic are visible. Mods in the MelonLoader folder were not in the scoped game folder and were not analyzed. Assertions marked inferred should be verified in-game or with the mod code.

LESSONS
- Give each pistol several discrete grab zones (grip, slide, magazine, rear sight) with separate hand poses, pose radii and strength or break values, following the BoxGrip and BarrelGrip zone model. Choose the zone each frame from hand position (CheckZones pattern).
- Pick grab candidates by a per-grip score function (ValidateGripScore) with a priority tie-break, instead of nearest collider. Let each weapon part override its scoring, as GenericGrip does with a surface raycast.
- Use a two-stage attach: a short timed snatch that eases the hand from its current pose to the grip target with accel and speed limits (CoMoveHandToTarget style), plus a quick-grab flag that skips the ease. The eased move is most of what makes a grab feel satisfying.
- Make release soft: ramp the hold joint's drive down over a short duration before destroying the joint (CoDelayDestroyJoint pattern), and keep a separate quick-release path.
- Compute throw velocity from an averaged, slightly delayed window of past frames (SaveHistory / GetAverageHistory pattern), not the last frame, to avoid jitter.
- For the pistol slide and thumb-push idea: model the slide as a one-axis constrained slide with min and max travel and a release threshold (slideIsPulled / isSlideReleased). Swap the hand pose when the slide is flipped (isFlippable + flippedHandPose / normalHandPose).
- Magazine grip: make its priority a function of state. It should be preferred only when out of the gun and should scale with ammo count (MagReleaseToGrab pattern).
- Use animation curves for friction and sweet spots (SweetGrabCurve, PositionFrictionCurve) rather than constants, and use rotation limits with a priority buffer so the hand can't spin freely.
- For two-hand holds: use primary, secondary and override roles, plus a hook that lets the primary grip compute the object pose from both hands (CalculateFinalOverride), instead of fighting two joints.
- Force pull: far hover, then a dwell state, then a pull with capped force and speed through a temporary joint, then a normal snatch on arrival. Pre-curl the fingers while the object flies in. Usable for pulling a pistol off a holster.
- Track per-hand, per-grip state as a small bitmask (HOVER, FARHOVER, ATTACH, PULLING) so the hover and attach logic stays a simple state machine.
- Caveat: every behavior above is inferred from names and signatures. Test feel in the real game or a mod before copying the design, and do not copy the names as if they were verified code.

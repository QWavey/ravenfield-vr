#2 KeepOffGun overwrites the glove keep-out written by KeepOutOfGun in the same frame
VERDICT: CONFIRMED (confidence medium)

- ReloadInteractions.cs:1613 then calls FollowHand, and MagazineProp.cs:1204-1208 writes HeldBy.Model.KeepOut = _keptOut whenever _keptOut is non-zero. The early return at 1204-1205 means there is no overwrite when the magazine is free.

- Fix idea: combine the two (per-component max along insert), or skip the write in KeepOffGun when Model.KeepOutFrame == Time.frameCount.

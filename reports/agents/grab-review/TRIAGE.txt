Grab-picture review, 2026-10-15: triage of the Haiku reviewers' findings
=========================================================================

Run: tools/vrshots.py --weapons (full run, 18:49), 233 weapon sheets sorted by kind (tools/shots_categories.py),
two Haiku reviewers per category, read only (workflow wf_da78a3a5-2a8, 15 agents). Their reports: <category>.txt here.
256 findings on 136 of 233 sheets. Each finding was checked against the pictures and the autotest log, grouped by root cause, and fixed
at the cause. Re-checked with targeted runs (vrshots --weapon ...).

ROOT CAUSES FOUND
-----------------

1. Picture tool: the free glove stood in front of the magazine close-ups (mag 1-3)               ~70 findings
   (PATRIOT, AK-24, asval, ak12 family, xm8, M4A1, QBZ192/HK416, CZ Bren 2, vector, GREASER, PATRIOT GL...)
   Fix: the free hand is moved out of the side camera's way for those pictures (commit 45a1246, after the run).
   Checked: BRN180 SS, M4A1, asval sheets now show the gun and magazine.

2. Picture tool: "lined up under the well" framed too low: the fresh magazine waits a magazine's   ~45 findings
   length under the well, only its tip showed at the frame's bottom edge (reviewers: "magazine lies on the ground").
   Fix: that picture is aimed at the fresh magazine's top, from 50 cm. Checked: M4A1, HK416, BRN180.

3. BRN180 family (16 weapons): every magazine close-up showed a wall                                ~45 findings
   Cause: the BRN 180 parks spent rounds 25 m away; the magazine's measured middle was 12 m off, the camera aimed
   there. Fix: DrawnBounds leaves out pieces more than a metre from the rest. Checked: BRN180 SS, G BRN180 EO S.
   (The "see-through receiver" of the G / Gear variants is the mod's own glass skin, not a bug.)

4. K13 family: a solid black arc over the receiver in the right-side pictures                       10 findings
   Cause: the scope lens disc measured as wide as the receiver. Fix: lens disc kept to an ocular's size (80b07eb).
   Checked: K13 ACOG.

5. ARS Glock 19 / HK416 / MP9: no magazine pictures at all                                         3 findings
   Cause: their magazine is a bone of the gun's skinned mesh, the check stopped ("draws nothing of its own").
   Fix: the analysed magazine box stands in; the pictures are taken. Checked: all three have mag 1-7 now.

6. AS Val (mod "asval", 4 variants), ak12h 1p87: the racking hand floated over the front sight       ~12 findings
   Cause: the rack point came from a stroke of the reload animation's hand that passed the gun 10-12 cm up, at the
   front sight (the gun itself tilts 31 degrees in that reload). Fix: a reload-hand stroke that starts more than 5 cm
   off the measured model is no rack: the estimate above the grip is used, put on the receiver. The surface snap now
   searches all the way to the bore (it stopped at 6 cm, so MK46's point stayed 15 cm up). The rack check waits for
   the gun's measurement before placing the hand (it pictured the old point). Checked: MK46 glove now on the cover.

7. Akimbo Berettas (AA-AA, AUTOMATICO AKIMBO): magazine close-ups one flat colour                    6 findings
   Cause: the left-side camera sat inside the other pistol's (stretched skinned) mesh. Fix: a picture that is one
   flat colour is taken again from the gun's other side. Checked: both sheets show the gun and magazine.

8. MW M870 (No script): two shotgun shells hanging in the air beside the gun                         1 finding
   Cause: the mod's reload animation keeps its two shells ("zidan") beside the gun at rest, out of the flat view.
   Fix: small parts the reload carries far that wait outside the gun at rest are not drawn while held (like the
   spare magazines). One of the two was gone in the check run; the other waited within the 4 cm margin given to the
   gun's box, now 1 cm: to check in the next run.

9. RPG-7V variants and Panzerfaust 3: "did not fire" although the game says it can                   (autotest)
   Cause: their draw time (the game's unholster time) is longer than the 3 s the test waited, and CanFire() does not
   look at it; the game's draw delay is right, the test was not. Fix: the test waits out the draw time. Checked: both
   fire now (no longer listed).

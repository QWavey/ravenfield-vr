#2 [minor] [confidence high] Sprint toggle pressed while standing still cancels itself in the same frame and clears crouch/prone
   where: src/Input/GameInputBridge.cs:302-316 (clears at 307-308, cancel at 312-316)
   trigger: default SprintMode.Toggle (VRConfig.cs:184); the stick has been idle for more than 0.35 s; the player presses Sprint.
   effect: SprintActive goes true then false in the same frame, so the press does nothing. CrouchToggled and ProneToggled are cleared on the way, so a crouched player stands up for nothing.
   evidence: 304-308 toggle sprint on and clear the stance toggles. 312-316 cancel sprint when _idleSince is older than 0.35 s. _idleSince only moves while the stick is pushed (320-322), so a stationary press is cancelled at once.
   fix idea: reset _idleSince when the toggle turns sprint on, and clear the stance toggles only once sprint is actually moving.

#3 [minor] [confidence medium] A targeted tap on left shift becomes a broadcast hold while sprinting
   where: src/Input/ScriptKeys.cs:93-118 (SetHeld; the reset at 111-117 and Target=null at 115); src/Core/VRManager.cs:159; src/UI/ModKeysPanel.cs:193
   trigger: sprinting on foot in toggle mode; the player taps a Mod keys panel entry for a script that polls left shift (ScriptKeys.Tap with a target).
   effect: VRManager calls SetHeld("left shift", true) every frame while sprinting. With a tap in flight, SetHeld does not return early (ReleaseAt != float.MaxValue), so it clears Target, refreshes ChangeFrame and resets the delivery maps. The tap is then delivered to every script and held until sprint ends, which defeats the single-target design described at ScriptKeys.cs:33-35.
   evidence: SetHeld lines 103-110 return early only when no tap is in flight; lines 111-117 unconditionally reset Target and ChangeFrame.
   fix idea: in SetHeld, when a tap is in flight, leave Target and ReleaseAt alone; or give the sprint hold its own VirtualKey so it cannot overwrite a tap.

   evidence: Slot swaps Fire<->Use and SlideRelease<->Squad, MagRelease<->Map (VRInput.cs:614-619) regardless of binding mode; Squad's any-hand read goes through Slot (VRInput.cs:385).
   fix idea: confirm complex_button single/double/long semantics in SteamVR; if they are momentary, bind the held actions (Squad, Use) as plain click outputs on both sides, or keep hold gestures on the same mode in both layouts.

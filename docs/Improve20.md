# /improve 20 — round log

Baseline: the VR quick autotest on commit 95349c0 + the measurement block ("Measurements" at the top of
BepInEx/RavenfieldVR-autotest.txt). The rounds were built while the game could not be launched and measured in the
next test session (VR quick runs 20:48 - 21:12); each round carries its own in-run comparison where possible.

Net, start to finish (VR quick run, 60 weapons): hand points buried in what they hold 12.4 -> 1.0 average for the gun
hand (posing the old way vs now, same run); support hand round a pistol's gun hand 13.1 -> 0.6; hand cost per frame
0.100 -> 0.025 ms; drops 15.6 -> 3.9 ms; physics shapes of unreadable weapons 64% -> 36% outside the model and 50% ->
83% of it covered; problems 6 -> 2 (the 2 left are a weapon mod's own laser script). 19 of 20 rounds kept with a
measured gain, 1 inconclusive (6).
